Pre Matric Scholarship MP 2026 — Class 9 & 10 SC/ST Students

By NaukriBulletin Team

What is Pre Matric Scholarship?

Pre Matric Scholarship is for SC/ST students studying in Class 9 and Class 10 in Madhya Pradesh. It covers maintenance allowance and study expenses.

Eligibility

  • SC/ST students only (OBC pre-matric not under central scheme)
  • Class 9 or Class 10
  • Annual family income: Below ₹2.5 lakh
  • MP domicile
  • Studying in a government or government-aided school

Scholarship Amount

CategoryDay ScholarHosteller
SC/ST Class 9-10₹3,500/year₹7,000/year

(Additional book allowance and ad-hoc grant may be available)

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar card of student
  • Parents' Aadhaar cards
  • Samagra ID (student's individual + family)
  • Income certificate (below ₹2.5 lakh — valid, from Tehsil)
  • Caste certificate (SC/ST)
  • Domicile certificate (MP)
  • Previous year marksheet / school progress report
  • School enrollment certificate (current year)
  • Bank passbook (student's name, Aadhaar-linked)
  • Passport photograph

Application Process

1. Visit scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in
2. Register with Samagra ID + Aadhaar
3. Select Pre Matric Scholarship
4. Fill student and school details
5. Upload documents
6. Enter bank account (student's account)
7. Submit before deadline (usually October-November)
💡 For younger students: Bank account should be opened in student's name — minor accounts allowed at all banks with parent as guardian.

School's Role

Schools usually help students fill applications. If your school is not helping, apply yourself at the portal.

Check Status

scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in → "Application Status" → Enter Samagra ID
