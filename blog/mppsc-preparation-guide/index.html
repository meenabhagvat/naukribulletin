MPPSC 2026: Complete Preparation Guide — Syllabus, Books & Strategy

By NaukriBulletin Team

MPPSC Overview

MPPSC State Service Exam recruits Deputy Collectors, DSPs, and other Group A/B officers.

Exam Pattern

Prelims:

- Paper 1: General Studies — 200 marks, 100 questions, 2 hours

- Paper 2: General Aptitude — 200 marks, 100 questions, 2 hours

- Negative marking: 1/3 per wrong answer

Mains: 6 papers (GS I-IV + Hindi + Essay) = 1,800 marks + Interview 175 marks

Eligibility

- Age: 21-40 years (relaxation for SC/ST/OBC)

- Qualification: Any graduate

- Domicile: MP resident

Best Books

- NCERT Class 6-12 (History, Geography, Polity, Science)

- MP GK — Arihant Publications

- Indian Polity — M. Laxmikanth

- Modern History — Spectrum

- Current Affairs — Pratiyogita Darpan, Dainik Bhaskar

6-Month Strategy

- Month 1-2: NCERT basics

- Month 3-4: MP-specific topics (history, culture, geography)

- Month 5: Current affairs (last 12 months)

- Month 6: Mock tests daily + revision

Key Tips

  1. MP-specific questions carry heavy weightage
  2. Solve previous 10 years' papers
  3. Paper 2 (Aptitude) is scoring — practice daily
  4. Join a MPPSC mock test series
