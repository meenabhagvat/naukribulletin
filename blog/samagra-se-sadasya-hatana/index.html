Samagra Parivar Se Sadasya Kaise Hataye — Death or Migration 2026

By NaukriBulletin Team

When to Remove a Member from Samagra?

  • Death — Remove deceased member to stop fraudulent use of benefits
  • Migration — Member moved permanently to another district/state
  • Marriage — Daughter moved to husband's Samagra family
  • Duplicate entry — Member accidentally registered twice

Documents Required

For death:

  • Death certificate (from municipality / gram panchayat)
  • Family Samagra ID

For migration/marriage:

  • Address proof of new residence
  • Family Samagra ID
  • New family's Samagra ID (if transferring)

Online Process

1. Visit samagra.gov.in
2. Click "समग्र नागरिक सेवाएं" → "सदस्य हटाएं"
3. Enter Family Samagra ID
4. Verify with OTP
5. Select the member to remove
6. Select reason (Death / Migration / Marriage / Duplicate)
7. Upload supporting document
8. Submit — request goes for verification

Offline Process (Gram Panchayat)

1. Visit Gram Panchayat or Ward Office
2. Request member removal form
3. Fill reason and submit with documents
4. Official processes removal in 3-7 days

After Removing Deceased Member

  • Their Samagra ID becomes inactive
  • Any benefits linked to them stop
  • Death certificate should also be obtained for other purposes (insurance, property, bank)
💡 Important: Removing a deceased member is important — active Samagra IDs of deceased persons can be misused for scheme benefits.
