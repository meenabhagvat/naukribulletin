Samagra ID Mein Naam aur DOB Sudhar Kaise Kare 2026

By NaukriBulletin Team

Why Correct Samagra Name/DOB?

Your name in Samagra must match your Aadhaar card exactly. If there is a mismatch:

  • eKYC will fail
  • DBT money won't reach your account
  • Scholarship applications will be rejected
  • Ladli Behna benefit may be blocked

Documents Required

For name correction:

  • Aadhaar card (with correct name)
  • Samagra ID
  • School certificate / marksheet (if name differs from Aadhaar)

For DOB correction:

  • Birth certificate (municipality / gram panchayat)
  • Class 10 marksheet
  • Aadhaar card

Online Correction at samagra.gov.in

1. Visit samagra.gov.in
2. Click "समग्र प्रोफाइल अपडेट करें"
3. Enter your 9-digit Individual Samagra ID
4. Verify with OTP on registered mobile
5. Click on the field you want to correct (Name / DOB)
6. Enter correct information
7. Upload supporting document
8. Submit — request goes for verification

Verification Process

  • Your correction request goes to Gram Panchayat / Ward Office for approval
  • BLO (Booth Level Officer) or Panchayat Secretary verifies
  • Takes 3-7 working days
  • You get SMS confirmation when approved

Offline Correction (Gram Panchayat)

1. Visit Gram Panchayat (rural) or Ward Office (urban)
2. Fill Samagra correction form
3. Submit with supporting documents
4. Official submits correction in the system
5. Correction approved within 3-7 days

Check Correction Status

Visit samagra.gov.in → "आवेदन की स्थिति" → Enter your Samagra ID

💡 Important: After name correction in Samagra, redo your eKYC to relink Aadhaar with updated Samagra profile.
