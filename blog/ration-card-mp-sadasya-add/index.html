Ration Card Mein Naam Kaise Jodein MP 2026 — Add New Member

By NaukriBulletin Team

When to Add a Member to Ration Card?

  • Newborn child (add within 1 year)
  • Newly married wife (add after marriage)
  • Other family member moving in permanently

Documents Required

For newborn:

  • Birth certificate of child
  • Parents' Aadhaar cards
  • Existing ration card
  • Samagra ID of family

For newly married wife:

  • Marriage certificate
  • Wife's Aadhaar card
  • Deletion certificate from her previous ration card (if any)
  • Existing ration card

Online Process — rationmitra.nic.in

1. Visit rationmitra.nic.in
2. Login with mobile / Samagra ID
3. Select "राशन कार्ड में सदस्य जोड़ें" (Add Member)
4. Enter your Ration Card number
5. Fill new member's details: name, DOB, relation, Aadhaar
6. Upload documents
7. Submit — note application number
8. Track at rationmitra.nic.in → "Application Status"

Offline Process (Food Inspector / Ward Office)

1. Visit your local Food Inspector or Ward Office
2. Fill member addition form
3. Submit with all documents
4. Member added within 7-15 working days
5. Updated ration card issued or entries made in existing card

Processing Time

  • Online: 7-15 working days
  • Offline: 7-15 working days

After Addition

  • New member's name appears on ration card
  • They can collect ration from Fair Price Shop (FPS)
  • For Aadhaar-seeded ration cards — new member must also get Aadhaar seeded
💡 Note: Children below 5 years don't get separate ration quota but should be registered for future eligibility and Samagra benefits.

Helpline

MP Food Department: 181 (toll free)

Website: rationmitra.nic.in
