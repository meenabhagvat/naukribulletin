Aadhaar Address Update Online 2026 — Step by Step Guide

By NaukriBulletin Team

How to Update Aadhaar Address Online

You can update your Aadhaar address online through the myAadhaar portal if your mobile number is registered with Aadhaar.

Prerequisites

  • Mobile number registered with Aadhaar (for OTP)
  • Valid address proof document
  • Aadhaar number

Valid Address Proof Documents

  • Passport
  • Bank passbook / statement (not older than 3 months)
  • Electricity / water / gas bill (not older than 3 months)
  • Voter ID card
  • Driving licence
  • Rent agreement (notarised)
  • Government issued letter with address

Step-by-Step Process

1. Visit myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in
2. Click "Update Aadhaar" → "Update Demographics"
3. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
4. Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number
5. Select "Address" from the list of fields to update
6. Enter your new address carefully in all fields (house no., street, district, state, PIN)
7. Upload your address proof document (PDF/JPG, max 2MB)
8. Review details and click Submit
9. You will receive a URN (Update Request Number) — save it
10. Track status at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in → "Check Aadhaar Update Status"

Offline Process (If mobile not registered)

1. Visit your nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra or Post Office Aadhaar centre
2. Fill the Aadhaar Correction Form
3. Submit with original + self-attested copy of address proof
4. Biometric verification will be done
5. Pay ₹50 fee (offline update)
6. Collect acknowledgement slip with URN

How Long Does It Take?

  • Online update: 2-5 working days
  • Offline update: 7-10 working days
💡 Tip: Check your status using the URN on the UIDAI website. SMS alerts are sent to your registered mobile.

Fee

  • Online update: Free
  • Offline (Aadhaar centre): ₹50

Helpline

UIDAI Toll Free: 1947

Email: help@uidai.gov.in
