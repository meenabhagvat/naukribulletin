Samagra eKYC Kaise Kare 2026 — Aadhaar Se Link Karna

By NaukriBulletin Team

What is Samagra eKYC?

Samagra eKYC is the process of linking your Aadhaar number with your Samagra ID. It verifies your identity and activates your Samagra profile for government scheme benefits.

Why is eKYC Mandatory?

  • Required for Ladli Behna Yojana registration
  • Required for DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer)
  • Required for Sambal Yojana
  • Required for scholarships and pension schemes
  • Without eKYC, benefits may not reach your bank account

Prerequisites

  • Samagra Individual ID (9-digit)
  • Aadhaar card
  • Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP)

Online eKYC Process

1. Visit samagra.gov.in
2. Click "e-KYC करें" (eKYC)
3. Enter your 9-digit Individual Samagra ID
4. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
5. Click "OTP से eKYC करें"
6. Enter OTP received on Aadhaar-linked mobile
7. eKYC complete — you'll see a success message

If Mobile Not Linked to Aadhaar

You must first get your mobile linked to Aadhaar (offline at Aadhaar Seva Kendra), then do eKYC.

Alternatively, visit your Gram Panchayat/Ward office — they can do eKYC using biometric devices.

Offline eKYC at Gram Panchayat

1. Visit Gram Panchayat (rural) or Ward Office (urban) or Jan Seva Kendra
2. Carry Aadhaar card + Samagra ID
3. Operator will do biometric eKYC using fingerprint
4. Completely free

Check eKYC Status

1. Visit samagra.gov.in
2. Search your Samagra ID
3. Profile will show eKYC status as "सत्यापित" (Verified)
💡 Tip: After eKYC, your name in Samagra must match your Aadhaar exactly. If there's a mismatch, get it corrected first.
