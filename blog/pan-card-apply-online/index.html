PAN Card Apply Online 2026 — Free Instant e-PAN in 10 Minutes

By NaukriBulletin Team

Two Ways to Get PAN Card

Option 1: Instant e-PAN (Free — via Aadhaar)

  • Completely free
  • Done in 10 minutes online
  • Valid for all purposes (PDF format — can be printed)
  • Requires Aadhaar with linked mobile number

Option 2: Physical PAN Card (₹107 — via NSDL/UTIITSL)

  • Physical card delivered at address in 15-20 days
  • ₹107 fee (₹1,020 for foreign address)
  • Requires documents upload

Instant e-PAN via Income Tax Portal (Free)

1. Visit incometaxindiaefiling.gov.in → "Instant e-PAN"
2. Click "Get New e-PAN"
3. Enter your Aadhaar number
4. Enter OTP received on Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Verify details shown from Aadhaar
6. e-PAN generated — download PDF immediately
7. e-PAN number starts with your initials
💡 Instant e-PAN is 100% valid for opening bank accounts, income tax filing, investments etc.

Physical PAN via NSDL

1. Visit tin-nsdl.com → "Apply Online" → "New PAN"
2. Select Form 49A (for Indian citizens)
3. Fill personal details carefully
4. Upload documents:
  • Identity proof (Aadhaar)
  • Address proof
  • Date of birth proof
  • Photograph
    • 5. Pay ₹107 online
    6. Submit and note 15-digit Application ID
    7. PAN card delivered in 15-20 days

PAN via UTIITSL

1. Visit utiitsl.com → "PAN Card Services" → "Apply for New PAN"
2. Similar process to NSDL
3. Same fee ₹107

Check PAN Status

  • NSDL: tin-nsdl.com → "Know PAN Status"
  • UTIITSL: utiitsl.com → "Track PAN Application"

Link PAN to Aadhaar (Mandatory)

All PANs must be linked to Aadhaar. Unlinked PANs become inoperative.

1. Visit incometaxindiaefiling.gov.in
2. Login → "Profile" → "Link Aadhaar"
3. Enter Aadhaar and verify
4. Pay ₹1,000 penalty fee if linking after deadline
