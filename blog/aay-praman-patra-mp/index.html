Income Certificate MP 2026 — Apply Online & Offline

By NaukriBulletin Team

What is Income Certificate?

An income certificate (Aay Praman Patra) officially certifies your annual family income. It is required for:

  • Scholarship applications (post-matric, pre-matric, Medhavi)
  • BPL ration card
  • EWS certificate (Economically Weaker Section)
  • Fee concession in colleges
  • Government scheme benefits (Ladli Behna, housing etc.)

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Samagra ID
  • Self-declaration of income (in prescribed format)
  • Salary slip (if employed)
  • Land records (if farmer)
  • Bank statements (last 3-6 months)
  • Residence proof
  • Ration card (for family details)
  • Passport-size photograph

Online Apply — MP e-District

1. Visit mpedistrict.gov.in
2. Login with mobile
3. Search "आय प्रमाण पत्र" (Income Certificate)
4. Fill form with:
  • Family income details (all sources)
  • Occupation details
  • Family members and their income
    • 5. Upload documents
    6. Pay fee (₹20-50)
    7. Submit and save Application ID

Processing Time

  • Normal: 7-15 working days
  • Tatkal (urgent): 3-5 working days

Validity

  • Income certificate is generally valid for 1 year from date of issue
  • Must be renewed annually for scholarship applications

Income Limits to Know

For common schemes:

  • Ladli Behna Yojana: Below ₹2.5 lakh/year
  • Post-matric scholarship: Below ₹2.5 lakh/year (SC/ST), ₹1.5 lakh (OBC)
  • EWS category: Below ₹8 lakh/year
💡 Tip: The income certificate should reflect honest income. False declaration can lead to cancellation of benefits.

Fee: ₹20-50
