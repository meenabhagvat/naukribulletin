Ladli Behna Yojana: How to Apply, Eligibility & Documents

By NaukriBulletin Team

What is Ladli Behna Yojana?

Ladli Behna Yojana is a flagship scheme by the Madhya Pradesh government providing ₹1,250/month to eligible married women.

Eligibility

- Female, aged 23-60 years

- Married, widowed, or divorced

- MP resident with annual family income below ₹2.5 lakh

- Family must not own more than 5 acres of agricultural land

- No government employee or income tax payer in the family

Documents Required

  1. Aadhaar card (linked to mobile number)
  2. Samagra ID (family + individual)
  3. Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)
  4. Passport-size photograph

How to Apply

Step 1: Visit your nearest Gram Panchayat, Ward Office, or government camp.

Step 2: Fill the application form with Aadhaar number, Samagra ID, bank account number.

Step 3: Submit documents to the official.

Step 4: After verification, ₹1,250 will be credited to your bank account monthly.

Check Status

Visit cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in → "आवेदन की स्थिति" → Enter Aadhaar or application number.

Important

- The scheme is completely free — do not pay any agent

- Helpline: 0755-2700800

FAQ

Q: Can unmarried women apply?

No — only married, widowed, or divorced women are eligible.

Q: What if my Aadhaar is not linked to bank?

Get it linked at any bank branch or Aadhaar service centre first.
