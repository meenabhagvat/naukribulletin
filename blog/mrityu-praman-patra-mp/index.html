Mrityu Praman Patra (Death Certificate) MP 2026 — Apply Online

By NaukriBulletin Team

Why is Death Certificate Needed?

  • Insurance claim
  • Bank account closure / transfer
  • Property transfer / inheritance
  • Pension — to stop payment or transfer to spouse
  • Samagra — to remove deceased member
  • Widow pension application
  • Marriage dissolution

Registration Timeline

  • Within 21 days: Normal process, free
  • After 21 days: Delayed registration, SDM permission required

Documents Required

  • Hospital death certificate / death slip (if hospital death)
  • Aadhaar card of deceased
  • Aadhaar of applicant (family member)
  • Family Samagra ID
  • Address proof

For home death:

  • Declaration by family members
  • Witness declaration
  • Local authority (Patwari / Ward Member) recommendation

Online Apply

1. Visit crsorgi.gov.in or mpenagarpalika.gov.in
2. Register with mobile
3. Fill deceased's details: name, DOB, date of death, cause of death, place
4. Fill applicant details (family member)
5. Upload hospital death certificate / documents
6. Submit and get registration number
7. Collect death certificate from Ward Office / Gram Panchayat

Offline Apply

1. Visit Ward Office (urban) or Gram Panchayat (rural) within 21 days
2. Fill Form 2 (Death Registration Form)
3. Submit hospital death certificate + family documents
4. Certificate issued within 7 working days

Hospital Death

  • Hospital provides "Death Summary" / death slip
  • Take this to Ward Office / Gram Panchayat for official death certificate
  • Hospital slip alone is NOT a legal death certificate

Fee

  • Within 21 days: ₹5-10 (nominal)
  • After 21 days: ₹10-50
  • Duplicate: ₹20-50
