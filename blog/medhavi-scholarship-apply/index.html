Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana 2026 — Apply Online & Eligibility

By NaukriBulletin Team

What is Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana provides free college education to meritorious students of Madhya Pradesh whose families cannot afford higher education.

Who is Eligible?

  • Passed Class 12 from MP Board with 75% or more marks
  • OR passed Class 12 from CBSE/ICSE with 85% or more marks
  • Annual family income: Below ₹6 lakh
  • MP domicile
  • Admitted to government college in MP (or approved private college)

What is Covered?

  • Government colleges: Full tuition fee paid by government
  • Approved private colleges: Up to ₹1.5 lakh/year tuition fee paid
  • Courses covered: Engineering, Medical (MBBS), Law, Management, Arts, Science, Commerce
  • Duration: Full course duration (4 years for BE, 5.5 years for MBBS etc.)

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Samagra ID
  • Class 12 marksheet (75%+ MP Board or 85%+ CBSE)
  • Income certificate (below ₹6 lakh — from Tehsil)
  • Domicile certificate (MP)
  • College admission letter / fee receipt
  • Bank passbook (student's name, Aadhaar-linked)
  • Passport photograph

Application Process

1. Visit scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in
2. Register/Login with Samagra ID + Aadhaar
3. Select "Mukhyamantri Medhavi Vidyarthi Yojana"
4. Fill academic details — Class 12 marks, board, year
5. Fill college and course details
6. Upload all documents
7. Submit — college will verify and forward
8. Fee paid directly to college by government

Important Dates

  • Portal usually opens: July-August (after Class 12 results)
  • Application deadline: October-November
  • Apply as soon as college admission is confirmed

Check Application Status

scholarshipportal.mp.nic.in → Status → Samagra ID

💡 Tip: Even if you got admission in a private college, apply — government pays up to ₹1.5 lakh/year. Many students miss this because they think only government college students are eligible.
