Driving Licence MP Online Apply 2026 — Complete Step-by-Step Guide

By NaukriBulletin Team

Two Steps to Get Driving Licence

1. Learner's Licence (LL) — Apply first, give computer test
2. Permanent Driving Licence (DL) — Apply after 30 days, give driving test

Age Requirements

  • Motorcycle without gear: 16 years (with parent consent)
  • Motorcycle with gear / Car: 18 years
  • Commercial vehicle: 20 years

Documents Required

For Learner's Licence:

  • Aadhaar card (identity + address proof)
  • Date of birth proof (Aadhaar or Class 10 marksheet)
  • Passport-size photograph (digital — uploaded online)
  • Medical certificate (Form 1A) — for commercial vehicle

Step 1: Apply for Learner's Licence Online

1. Visit parivahan.gov.in
2. Click "Drivers/Learners Licence" → "Apply for Learner Licence"
3. Select Madhya Pradesh as state
4. Select your RTO (e.g., RTO Bhopal, RTO Indore)
5. Fill application — Aadhaar number, name, DOB, address
6. Upload photograph and documents
7. Pay fee online (₹200-500 depending on vehicle category)
8. Book slot for LL computer test at your RTO
9. Appear for test on selected date (30 MCQ questions — traffic rules)
10. If pass — Learner's Licence issued (smart card or PDF)

Learner's Licence Test Tips

  • 30 multiple choice questions on traffic rules and signs
  • Must score minimum 60% to pass
  • Study: Road signs, traffic rules, signal meanings
  • Practice at morth.nic.in → "LL Test Practice"

Step 2: Apply for Permanent Driving Licence

After 30 days (but before 180 days) of getting LL:

1. Visit parivahan.gov.in → "Apply for Driving Licence"
2. Select RTO and enter LL number
3. Fill DL application form
4. Upload documents
5. Pay fee (₹400-800)
6. Book slot for driving test at RTO
7. Appear for driving test in your vehicle (or RTO vehicle for a fee)
8. If pass — DL issued (smart card sent by post in 7-10 days)

Fee Structure

Track Application

Visit parivahan.gov.in → "Status of Application" → Enter Application Number
