Janm Praman Patra (Birth Certificate) MP 2026 — Apply Online

By NaukriBulletin Team

Why is Birth Certificate Needed?

Birth certificate is the most fundamental identity document:

  • School admission
  • Aadhaar card application for child
  • Passport
  • Voter ID age proof
  • Date of birth proof for all government documents
  • Marriage registration
  • Property / inheritance

Registration Timeline

  • Within 21 days of birth: Free registration, normal process
  • 21 days to 1 year: Delayed registration, requires Tehsil SDM permission (₹2 late fee)
  • After 1 year: Court order required (Magistrate), complex process
💡 Register within 21 days to avoid complications.

Hospital Birth (Most Common)

If born in hospital/nursing home:

1. Hospital itself registers the birth and provides Birth Notification
2. Take the hospital notification to your Ward Office (urban) or Gram Panchayat (rural)
3. Register and get Birth Certificate within 21 days

Documents Required

For hospital birth:

  • Hospital discharge slip / birth notification
  • Parents' Aadhaar cards
  • Address proof
  • Samagra ID of family

For home birth:

  • Declaration from traditional birth attendant (Dai) or witness
  • Parents' Aadhaar cards
  • Samagra ID

Online Apply — MP e-Nagar Palika / CRS Portal

1. Visit mpenagarpalika.gov.in (urban) or your district's CRS portal
2. Or visit crsorgi.gov.in (Civil Registration System)
3. Register with mobile
4. Fill child's details: name, DOB, place of birth, parents' details
5. Upload hospital birth notification / documents
6. Submit and get registration number
7. Collect certificate from Municipal Office / Gram Panchayat

Offline Apply

1. Visit Ward Office (urban) or Gram Panchayat (rural)
2. Fill Form 1 (Birth Registration Form)
3. Submit with documents
4. Certificate issued within 7 days (for timely registration)

Delayed Registration (After 21 days)

1. Visit SDM (Sub-Divisional Magistrate) office
2. Submit application with explanation for delay
3. SDM gives permission
4. Then register at Ward Office / Gram Panchayat
5. Additional fee: ₹2-10

Fee

  • Within 21 days: Free (or ₹5-10 nominal)
  • After 21 days: ₹10-50 (varies by district)
  • Duplicate certificate: ₹20-50

Helpline

MP e-Nagar Palika: 0755-2578900
