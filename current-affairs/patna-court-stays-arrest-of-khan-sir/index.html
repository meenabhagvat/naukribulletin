Patna Court Stays Arrest of Khan Sir

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Patna Court has stayed the arrest of 'Khan Sir', a prominent figure in the education sector. This development is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the importance of judicial intervention in protecting individual rights. The case also involves a rival coaching institute owner, Raushan Anand, who was earlier arrested.
