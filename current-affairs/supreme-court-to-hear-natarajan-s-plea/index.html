GOVERNMENT SCHEMES • 11 June 2026

Supreme Court to Hear Natarajan's Plea Against Rajya Sabha Nomination Rejection

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

The Supreme Court is set to hear a plea by Meenakshi Natarajan against the rejection of her Rajya Sabha nomination. This case is relevant for competitive exams as it deals with the electoral process and the role of the judiciary in India. The outcome of this case may have implications for the representation of various groups in the Rajya Sabha.
