Kollam Tourism Set for a Mega Makeover

Exam Relevance: UPSC / SSC / Banking / All

Summary

Kollam, a prominent tourist destination in Kerala, is undergoing significant development to enhance its tourism infrastructure. This initiative aims to boost local economy and attract more visitors, which is crucial for competitive exams focusing on economic development and tourism policies.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Kollam is known for its backwaters and historical significance.
  • The makeover includes improvements in facilities and attractions.
  • The initiative is part of Kerala's broader tourism development strategy.
