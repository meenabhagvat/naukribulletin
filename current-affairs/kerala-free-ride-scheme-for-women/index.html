Kerala Government's Free Ride Scheme for Women

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Kerala government's free ride scheme for women is expected to incur a significant revenue loss for the Kerala State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC). This scheme is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the challenges of implementing social welfare schemes while balancing the financial sustainability of public transport corporations. The scheme's impact on the state's economy and the KSRTC's finances will be a key aspect to focus on.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • ₹112 crore revenue loss for all categories of buses
  • ₹57 crore revenue loss for ordinary services
  • 90-day duration
