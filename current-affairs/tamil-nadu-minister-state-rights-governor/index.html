GOVERNMENT • 10 June 2026

Tamil Nadu Minister Advocates for State's Rights Against Governor's Powers

Exam Relevance: UPSC / SSC / Banking / All

Summary

Tamil Nadu's Higher Education Minister P. Viswanathan emphasized the need for harmony between the Governor and Chief Minister to ensure the smooth functioning of state universities. He asserted that the state cannot compromise on its rights against the Governor's attempts to usurp powers.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • P. Viswanathan is the Tamil Nadu Higher Education Minister.
  • The Minister calls for cooperation between the Governor and Chief Minister.
  • He warns against the Governor usurping state powers.
