Richest Candidate in Karnataka Council Election

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Karnataka Council election has seen the emergence of wealthy candidates, with Janata Dal (Secular) nominee Govindaraju being the richest. His total assets amount to ₹173.69 crore, making him a notable figure in the election. This news is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the intersection of politics and wealth in India.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Govindaraju's total assets are ₹173.69 crore
  • He is the richest candidate in the Karnataka Council election
  • Janata Dal (Secular) is his political party
