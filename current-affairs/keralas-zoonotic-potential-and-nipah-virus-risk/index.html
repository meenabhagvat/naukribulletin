Kerala's Zoonotic Potential and Nipah Virus Risk

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

The Nipah virus spillover risk in Kerala is highest from April to September due to increased bat foraging activity and viral shedding dynamics. This pattern has remained consistent since the first outbreak, making it crucial for competitive exam aspirants to understand the zoonotic potential and its implications. This knowledge can help in tackling questions related to environmental health and disease management.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Nipah virus spillover risk peak from April to September
  • Increased bat foraging activity and viral shedding dynamics
  • Kerala's zoonotic potential
