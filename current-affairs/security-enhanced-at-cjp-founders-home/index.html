Security Enhanced at CJP Founder's Home

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The security at the home of CJP founder Dipke in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar has been increased. This development is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the importance of law and order and the role of the police in maintaining security. It also showcases the government's efforts to ensure the safety of prominent individuals.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • 11 security personnel initially deployed
  • 15 personnel currently deployed
  • Security staff from local police station and city police headquarters
