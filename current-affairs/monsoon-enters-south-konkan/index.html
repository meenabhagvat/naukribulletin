ENVIRONMENT • 09 June 2026

Monsoon Enters South Konkan

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The monsoon has entered South Konkan, and the Agriculture and Disaster Management Department has forecast rainfall in parts of Sindhudurg and Ratnagiri districts. This development is crucial for farmers in the region, as it may impact their sowing schedules. The state government has advised farmers to defer sowing due to the expected rainfall.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Monsoon enters South Konkan
  • Rainfall forecast in Sindhudurg and Ratnagiri districts
  • Farmers advised to defer sowing
