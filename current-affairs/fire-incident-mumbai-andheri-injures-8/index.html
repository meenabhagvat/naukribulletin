ENVIRONMENT • 12 June 2026

Fire Incident in Mumbai's Andheri Injures 8

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

A Level-1 fire broke out in the Manisha Building near Arogya Nidhi Hospital in Andheri West, resulting in injuries to at least 8 individuals. The fire was contained to the AC unit on the 6th floor of the 11-storey building, highlighting the importance of fire safety measures in urban areas.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Fire occurred in Manisha Building, Andheri West
  • Injuries reported: at least 8 people
  • Fire confined to AC unit on the 6th floor
