Ladakh L-G to Lead Indian Delegation to Bring Buddha Relics Back from Mongolia

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

The Ladakh Lieutenant Governor will lead an Indian delegation to Mongolia to bring back Buddha relics. This visit follows the successful exposition of the sacred Piprahwa relics of Lord Buddha in Ladakh. The event highlights India's cultural ties with Mongolia and promotes Buddhist heritage.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Ladakh L-G leading delegation
  • Buddha relics from Mongolia
  • Piprahwa relics exposition in Ladakh
