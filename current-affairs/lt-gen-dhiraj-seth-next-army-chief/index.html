Lt Gen Dhiraj Seth to be next Army Chief

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

Lt Gen Dhiraj Seth has been named the next Chief of Army Staff, making him the first officer from the Armoured Corps to hold the position in three decades. This development is significant for competitive exams as it highlights the importance of defence and national security. Students should be aware of key appointments and their implications for India's defence strategy.
