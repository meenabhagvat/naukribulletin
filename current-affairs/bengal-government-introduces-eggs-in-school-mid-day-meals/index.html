Bengal Government Introduces Eggs in School Mid-Day Meals

By NaukriBulletin Editorial Team · Updated 02 August 2026

📚 Exam Relevance — Government Schemes
UPSCSSC CGLIBPS PO
Government schemes and their beneficiaries are asked in Welfare and Polity sections.

What happened

The West Bengal government has introduced eggs as part of the mid-day meal scheme for students in Kolkata schools. This move aims to improve the nutritional value of the meals provided to students. The scheme is expected to benefit around 44,000 students.
