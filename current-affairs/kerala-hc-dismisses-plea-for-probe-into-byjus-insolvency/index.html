Kerala HC Dismisses Plea for Probe into Byju's Insolvency

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Kerala High Court has dismissed a plea for a probe into irregularities in Byju's insolvency, which is a significant development in the ongoing legal battle between Byju's and Voizzit. This news is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the importance of corporate governance and regulatory oversight in the Indian economy. The case also underscores the role of investigative agencies like the CBI, ED, and NIA in preventing malpractices.
