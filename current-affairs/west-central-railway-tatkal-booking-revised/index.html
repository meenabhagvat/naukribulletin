West Central Railway Revises Tatkal Booking Process

By NaukriBulletin Editorial Team · Updated 27 July 2026

RRB NTPCRRB Group DUPSC

What happened

The West Central Railway has revised its tatkal ticket booking process, which will come into effect from August 1. This change aims to improve the efficiency and transparency of the booking system. Competitive exam aspirants should be aware of such updates in government services and policies.
