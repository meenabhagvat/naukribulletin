Bengaluru's KIA Ranked Third Most Family-Friendly Airport

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

Kempegowda International Airport (KIA) in Bengaluru has been ranked the world's third most family-friendly airport. This recognition is significant for India's aviation sector and highlights the country's efforts to improve airport infrastructure.
