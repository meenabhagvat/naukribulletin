Dudhwa Tigress Death Probe

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

The National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA) has sought a report on the sudden death of a tigress in Dudhwa. This incident highlights the importance of conservation efforts and the need for effective management of protected areas. The probe will help identify the causes of the tigress' death and inform future conservation strategies.
