GOVERNMENT SCHEMES • 28 June 2026

Kolkata Warehouse Collapse

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

A warehouse collapse in Kolkata's Taratala area has resulted in the death of three members of a family from Bihar. This incident highlights the need for better safety measures and regulations in workplaces, particularly in the unorganized sector. It is relevant for competitive exams as it relates to labor laws and worker safety.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Six members of a family from Bihar worked at the warehouse
  • Three members died in the collapse
  • Incident occurred in Kolkata's Taratala area
