ENVIRONMENT • 27 June 2026

New Railway Line Through MP Tiger Corridor Gets Forest Clearance

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

The in-principle forest clearance for a new railway line through the Satpura-Melghat tiger corridor in Madhya Pradesh has been granted. This development is significant for competitive exams as it highlights the balance between infrastructure development and environmental conservation. The project's impact on the tiger population and ecosystem will be a crucial aspect to consider.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Satpura-Melghat tiger corridor
  • Madhya Pradesh
  • in-principle forest clearance
