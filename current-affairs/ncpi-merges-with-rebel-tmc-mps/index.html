NCPI to Merge with Rebel TMC MPs

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Nationalist Congress Party of India (NCPI) is set to merge with rebel Trinamool Congress (TMC) MPs, with numbers potentially going up to 22. This development may have significant implications for the political landscape of West Bengal and the National Democratic Alliance (NDA). The merger is expected to strengthen the NDA under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Home Minister Amit Shah.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • NCPI to merge with rebel TMC MPs
  • Up to 22 MPs may join
  • Merger to strengthen NDA
