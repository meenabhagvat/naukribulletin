Bengaluru Garbage Crisis Worsens

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The irregular collection of garbage in Bengaluru has led to a significant increase in blackspots, with 5,445 active blackspots reported on June 7. This issue is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the importance of waste management and urban planning. The NammaKasa portal's data can be used to track and analyze the effectiveness of waste management strategies.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • 5,445 active blackspots in Bengaluru
  • Increase from 3,200 in just a month
  • NammaKasa portal tracks blackspots
