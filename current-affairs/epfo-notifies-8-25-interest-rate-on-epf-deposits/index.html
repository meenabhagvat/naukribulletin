EPFO Notifies 8.25% Interest Rate on EPF Deposits for FY 2025-26

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has notified an 8.25% interest rate on EPF deposits for the financial year 2025-26. This move is expected to benefit millions of subscribers and is a key development in the realm of social security and retirement savings. The interest rate is crucial for competitive exams as it reflects the government's policies on employee welfare and economic growth.
