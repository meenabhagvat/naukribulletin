Bengaluru's Football Culture Shift

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The football culture in Bengaluru has undergone a significant transformation, from being driven by Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) to the emergence of academies. This shift is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the changing dynamics of sports culture in India. It also underscores the importance of private initiatives in promoting sports development.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) drove football culture in Bengaluru from 1970s to 2000s
  • Emergence of academies replaced PSUs in promoting football
  • Bengaluru's football culture has undergone significant transformation
