Guwahati Police Seize 37 kg Gold

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Guwahati police have seized 37 kg of gold valued at ₹54 crore from a man from Maharashtra. The gold is suspected to have been smuggled by an international group linked to Myanmar and West Asia. This incident highlights the issue of gold smuggling and its impact on the Indian economy.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • 37 kg gold seized
  • Valued at ₹54 crore
  • Linked to international smuggling group
