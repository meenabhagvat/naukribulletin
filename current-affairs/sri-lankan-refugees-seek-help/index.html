Sri Lankan Refugees Seek Help from New Government

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

Sri Lankan refugees in India are facing difficulties due to lack of bank accounts and identity papers, making them vulnerable to exploitation by local agents. The new government in Tamil Nadu and the Centre are expected to provide tangible help to these refugees. This issue is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the challenges faced by refugees and the role of government in providing support.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Sri Lankan refugees in India
  • lack of bank accounts and identity papers
  • exploitation by local agents
