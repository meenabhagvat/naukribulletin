Uttar Pradesh to Host 250 MW Solar Power Plant on Vacant Defence Land

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Defence Ministry has announced the setup of a 250 MW solar power plant in Uttar Pradesh on vacant defence land, reflecting the government's commitment to clean energy and sustainability. This initiative is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the government's efforts towards reducing dependence on conventional energy sources. The project is also a step towards achieving India's renewable energy targets.
