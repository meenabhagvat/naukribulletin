NHAI Signs MoU with NCAER

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the National Council of Applied Economic Research (NCAER) to establish the NHAI Centre for Economics of Infrastructure. This partnership aims to promote research and analysis in the field of infrastructure development. The collaboration is expected to benefit competitive exam aspirants by providing insights into the government's initiatives in the infrastructure sector.
