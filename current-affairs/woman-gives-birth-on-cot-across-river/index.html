GOVERNMENT SCHEMES • 28 June 2026

Woman Gives Birth on Cot Across River Due to Lack of Ambulance and Bridge

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

A woman in Madhya Pradesh's Chhindwara district gave birth to a child while being carried on a cot across a river, highlighting the lack of basic healthcare facilities and infrastructure in rural areas. This incident is relevant for competitive exams as it raises questions about the effectiveness of government schemes aimed at improving healthcare and rural development. It also underscores the need for better implementation of policies and allocation of resources.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Lack of ambulance
  • No bridge across the river
  • Rural healthcare infrastructure
