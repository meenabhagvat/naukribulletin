GOVERNMENT SCHEMES • 26 June 2026

India's Toshakhana to Auction Exotic Items

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) is auctioning off over 300 exotic items from its toshakhana, including Rolex watches, gold jewellery, and Omani daggers. This move is expected to generate revenue for the government and provide a unique opportunity for the public to own rare items. The auction is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the government's efforts to monetize its assets and promote transparency.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Over 300 items up for auction
  • MEA's toshakhana collection
  • Revenue generation for government
