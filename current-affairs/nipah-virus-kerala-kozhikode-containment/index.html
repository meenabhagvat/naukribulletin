Containment Steps in Kerala's Kozhikode After Nipah Virus Case

Exam Relevance: UPSC / SSC / Banking / All

Summary

A man in Kozhikode has tested positive for the Nipah virus, prompting containment measures by the health department. Seventy-seven individuals on the patient's contact list are being monitored, with 15 people currently quarantined.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Nipah virus case confirmed in Kozhikode, Kerala.
  • 77 individuals identified as contacts of the patient.
  • 15 people have been quarantined as a precaution.
