Western Ghats Eco-Sensitive Areas to be Notified

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

The government is set to notify eco-sensitive areas in the Western Ghats region across at least three states, providing protection to the biodiversity-rich area after a 13-year wait. This move is crucial for conservation efforts and has significant implications for competitive exams. The notification will help in regulating human activities in the region and preserving the ecosystem.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Western Ghats eco-sensitive areas
  • 13-year wait
  • 3 states
