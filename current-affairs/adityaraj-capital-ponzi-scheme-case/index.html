Adityaraj Capital Ponzi Scheme Case Transferred to CID

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Adityaraj Capital ponzi scheme case has been transferred to the CID by the Belagavi police due to the large amount of money involved. This case is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the issue of ponzi schemes and the role of regulatory bodies in preventing such scams. Aspirants should be aware of the measures taken by law enforcement agencies to combat economic crimes.
