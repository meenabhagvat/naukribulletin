Chinese Firms Allowed to Bid for Power Projects in India

By NaukriBulletin Editorial Team · Updated 03 July 2026

📚 Exam Relevance — General Awareness
UPSCSSC CGLIBPS PO
Current affairs from all domains appear in the GA sections of SSC, Banking and UPSC exams.

What happened

The Indian government has given a two-year exemption to four Chinese companies with factories in India, allowing them to bid for power projects. This move is significant for competitive exams as it highlights the government's approach to handling trade relations with China. It also underscores the importance of understanding India's economic policies and their implications.
