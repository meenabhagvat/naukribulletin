Karnataka's First Government Hospital to Perform Robot-Assisted Total Knee Replacement

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

Karnataka Medical College and Research Institute in Hubballi has successfully performed a robot-assisted total knee replacement, a first for a government hospital in the state. This achievement sets a new benchmark for advanced orthopaedic care in the public health sector. It highlights the growing adoption of technology in healthcare, particularly in government institutions.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • First government hospital in Karnataka to perform robot-assisted total knee replacement
  • Karnataka Medical College and Research Institute in Hubballi
  • Advanced orthopaedic care in public health sector
