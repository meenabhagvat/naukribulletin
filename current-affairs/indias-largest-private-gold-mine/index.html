India's Largest Private Gold Mine to Yield 1 Tonne of Gold Annually

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Jonnagiri gold mine in Andhra Pradesh is set to produce 1 tonne of gold per year, with an estimated 12 tonnes of gold reserves. This development is expected to generate significant revenue for the state government through a 4% royalty. The project's success can have a positive impact on India's economy and gold production.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • 12 tonnes of gold reserves
  • 1 tonne of gold production per year
  • 4% royalty to Andhra Pradesh government
