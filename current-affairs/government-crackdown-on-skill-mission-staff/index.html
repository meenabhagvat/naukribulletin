GOVERNMENT SCHEMES • 22 June 2026

Government Cracks Down on Skill Mission Staff

Exam Relevance: UPSC

Summary

The government has issued notices to 20 staff members at the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) for major lapses in the Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) scheme. This move is part of a larger crackdown on corruption and inefficiency in government-run skill development programs. The development is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the government's efforts to improve skill development and vocational training in India.
