Heavy Rain Alert in Andhra Pradesh

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority has issued a heavy rain alert for Rayalaseema and South Coastal A.P. on June 9 and 10. This warning is crucial for competitive exam students to be aware of the current weather conditions and their potential impact on the region. The alert also highlights the importance of disaster management in India.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Heavy rain alert in Rayalaseema and South Coastal A.P.
  • Day temperatures between 42°C and 43°C expected
  • Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority issued the alert
