APSRTC Employees Protest Against Electric Bus Privatisation

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (APSRTC) employees' unions are protesting against the government's plans to hand over electric buses and bus depots to private operators on lease. This move is seen as a step towards privatisation, which could impact the livelihoods of APSRTC employees. The protest is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the issues related to public sector undertakings and privatisation.
