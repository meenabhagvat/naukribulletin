CtrlS Secures ₹7,000 cr Investment from Canada Pension Plan

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

CtrlS, a Hyderabad-based company, has secured a significant investment of ₹7,000 crore from the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board. This investment will enable CtrlS to develop hyperscale data centre campuses across India, boosting the country's digital infrastructure. The deal highlights the growing interest of foreign investors in India's digital sector.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • ₹7,000 cr investment
  • 8.2% stake for CPP Investments
  • Hyperscale data centre campuses across India
