Heavy Rains in Cyberabad

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

Heavy rains have caused waterlogging and traffic snarls in several areas of Cyberabad. This news is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the impact of climate change and extreme weather events on urban infrastructure. Students should be aware of the effects of heavy rainfall on cities and the measures taken to mitigate them.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Rajendranagar recorded 55 mm rainfall
  • Waterlogging and traffic snarls reported
  • Telangana Development Planning Society tracked rainfall data
