GOVERNMENT SCHEMES • 09 June 2026

Tamil Nadu Govt Urged to Support Senior Citizens

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Tamil Nadu government has been urged to provide safe shelter, food, and medical care to senior citizens. This comes after an incident in Namakkal where three senior citizens ended their lives due to lack of support. The issue highlights the need for effective implementation of government schemes for the elderly.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Tamilisai Soundararajan urged CM Vijay to support senior citizens
  • Incident in Namakkal led to the appeal
  • Government schemes for elderly need effective implementation
