Vijayawada Waterlogging

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

Overnight rain in Vijayawada has caused waterlogging in major roads and low-lying areas, affecting daily life. This incident highlights the need for effective urban planning and disaster management. Competitive exam students should be aware of such environmental issues and their impact on cities.

📌 Key Facts for Exam

  • Vijayawada waterlogged
  • Low-lying areas affected
  • NTR Circle and Pandit Nehru Bus Station impacted
