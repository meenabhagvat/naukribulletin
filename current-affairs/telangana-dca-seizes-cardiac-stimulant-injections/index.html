Telangana DCA Seizes Cardiac Stimulant Injections

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Telangana Drugs Control Administration (DCA) has seized cardiac stimulant injections being sold to gym-goers in Warangal. This action is relevant for competitive exams as it highlights the importance of regulatory bodies in ensuring public health and safety. The incident also underscores the need for awareness about the misuse of prescription drugs.
