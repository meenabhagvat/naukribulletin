Tirupati Health Department Prepares for Monsoon

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

The Principal Secretary has reviewed the preparedness of the Tirupati health department ahead of the monsoon season. This move is crucial to prevent water-borne diseases and ensure public health during the rainy season. The review highlights the government's efforts to mitigate the impact of natural disasters on public health.
