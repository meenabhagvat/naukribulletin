Tamil Nadu CM Proposes Class 12 Marks for MBBS Admissions

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay has suggested that MBBS admissions should be based on Class 12 marks. This proposal is significant for competitive exam students as it may impact the medical entrance exam landscape. The move aims to reduce the stress associated with entrance exams and promote a more holistic approach to admissions.
