AI-Generated NEET Questions Sold on Instagram

Exam Relevance: All

Summary

A law student from Indore was arrested for selling AI-generated questions as genuine NEET papers on Instagram. This incident highlights the growing concern of AI-generated content being misused for financial gain. Competitive exam aspirants should be aware of such scams and rely on authentic sources for study materials.
