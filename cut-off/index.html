Government Exam Cut-Off Marks 2026

A cut-off is the minimum score needed to qualify a stage, set separately for each category (UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS). This page explains how to read cut-offs, what drives them, and links the official portals.

Always check cut-offs only on the official website. Beware of fake links on social media.

How to check your cut-off

  1. Open the official result or notice section of the recruiting body (links below).
  2. Find the cut-off PDF released with the result for your exam and year.
  3. Locate your category (UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS) and, where relevant, your post and region.
  4. Compare with previous years to gauge the trend.
  5. Use the cut-off to judge whether your estimated score from the answer key qualifies.

What you'll need

  • Your category
  • Your estimated or actual score
  • The post / region you applied for
  • Previous-year cut-offs for context

Frequently asked questions

What decides the cut-off?+

Number of vacancies, number and quality of candidates, paper difficulty, and category-wise reservation.

Why is the cut-off different for each category?+

Cut-offs are set separately for UR, OBC, SC, ST and EWS as per reservation policy.

Are expected cut-offs reliable?+

They are informed estimates based on difficulty and past trends — useful for planning, but the official cut-off is final.

Where is the official cut-off published?+

With the result PDF on the recruiting body's website.
