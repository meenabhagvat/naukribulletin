About Cut-off Marks

Cut-off marks are the minimum qualifying marks required to pass each stage of a government exam. Cut-offs vary by category — General candidates typically face the highest cut-offs, while SC, ST, OBC and EWS candidates have lower cut-offs as per reservation policy. Use this tracker to compare cut-offs across years and plan your target accordingly.
