Government Exam Cut-Off Marks 2026

A cut-off is the minimum score needed to qualify a stage or be selected, set separately for each category (UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS) and often each post and region. This page explains what decides a cut-off, how to read previous-year trends, and links the official portals where final cut-offs are published.

By NaukriBulletin Editorial Team
Heads up: Always check cut-offs only on the official website of the recruiting body. Beware of fake links on social media. The official portals are listed below.

How to check your cut-off (step by step)

  1. Open the official result or notice section of the recruiting body (links below).
  2. Find the cut-off PDF released along with the result for your exam and year.
  3. Locate your category (UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS) and, where relevant, your post and region.
  4. Compare with previous years to gauge the trend for your exam.
  5. Use the cut-off to judge whether your estimated score (from the answer key) qualifies.

What you'll need

  • Your category
  • Your estimated or actual score
  • The post / region you applied for
  • Previous-year cut-offs for context

Frequently asked questions

What decides the cut-off?

Mainly the number of vacancies, the number and quality of candidates, the difficulty of the paper, and category-wise reservation. Easier papers and fewer vacancies push cut-offs up.

Why is the cut-off different for each category?

Cut-offs are set separately for UR, OBC, SC, ST and EWS as per reservation policy, so the qualifying mark differs by category.

Are expected cut-offs reliable?

They are informed estimates based on difficulty and past trends — useful for planning, but the official cut-off published with the result is final.

Where is the official cut-off published?

With the result PDF on the recruiting body's website. We summarise category-wise cut-offs as they are released.
