Preparation Strategy

  1. SBI PO Mains has the toughest DI in banking exams — dedicate 2 hours daily to it.
  2. Group Exercise tests leadership and communication — practise group discussions.
  3. SBI expects candidates to know SBI-specific products, schemes and history.
  4. Descriptive writing (essay + letter) carries 50 marks — do not ignore.
