Preparation Strategy

  1. Focus on Maths and Reasoning — these are highly scoring and formulaic.
  2. Railway-specific GK (history of Indian Railways, zones, rail ministers) is important.
  3. CBT 1 is a screening test — aim for top 20× the vacancies in your zone.
  4. Practise typing (WPM requirement varies by post) early if applying for clerical posts.
