Preparation Strategy

  1. Prelims needs speed — solve 100 Qs in 60 min. Practice sectional time management.
  2. Puzzles and DI are scoring in Mains — do 5 sets daily.
  3. Read banking news daily: RBI announcements, repo rate, new schemes, bank mergers.
  4. English descriptive (essay + letter) in Mains is often ignored — practise weekly.
  5. Attempt mock tests on IBPS official pattern and analyse each test thoroughly.
