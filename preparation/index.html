Quick tips for all exams

  • Start with NCERT books for static GK — they are the base for SSC, UPSC and Banking.
  • Solve at least 5 previous year papers before your exam.
  • Negative marking is real — only attempt questions you are 70%+ confident about.
  • Current affairs from the last 6 months are critical for all competitive exams.
  • Time management in the exam is a skill — practise it in every mock test.
