Preparation Strategy

  1. Start with NCERT books for GK foundation (Class 6–12 History, Geography, Polity, Economy).
  2. Solve 50 Reasoning questions daily — speed is key in Tier 1.
  3. Do previous year papers from 2017 onwards to understand question patterns.
  4. Target 160+ in Tier 1 for a safe qualifying score in UR category.
  5. Tier 2 Maths and English are scoring — dedicate 60% of prep time here.
