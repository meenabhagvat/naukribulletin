Government Exam Admit Cards 2026

An admit card is the document you must carry to the exam centre. It confirms your eligibility and shows your exam date, time, centre and instructions. Download only from the official website.

🔔 Get Free Alerts Practice Mock Tests
Always check admit cards only on the official website. Beware of fake links on social media.

How to check your admit card

  1. Visit the official website of the recruiting body (links below) 1–2 weeks before the exam.
  2. Open the 'Admit Card' or 'Download Call Letter' section.
  3. Log in with your registration number and password, or roll number and date of birth.
  4. Check every detail — name, photo, exam date, reporting time and centre.
  5. Download and take at least two colour printouts. Carry one with a valid photo ID on exam day.

What you'll need

  • Registration number & password
  • Roll number / date of birth
  • A printer for a hard copy
  • A valid original photo ID for exam day

Frequently asked questions

When is the admit card released?+

Usually 1–2 weeks before the exam date. We update this page as soon as the admit card is out.

I forgot my registration number — what now?+

Use the 'Forgot Registration No./Password' link on the official login page and recover it with your registered email or mobile.

There is a mistake on my admit card — can I fix it?+

Minor errors may be correctable through the official correction window. Act immediately and keep proof of your application.

What must I carry to the exam centre?+

A printed admit card and an original valid photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Voter ID). Follow the dress and item rules on the admit card.
