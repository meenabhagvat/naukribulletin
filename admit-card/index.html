Government Exam Admit Cards 2026

An admit card (hall ticket) is the document you must carry to the exam centre — it confirms your eligibility and shows your exam date, time, centre and instructions. This page explains how to download your admit card safely, links every major official portal, and is updated as admit cards are released.

By NaukriBulletin Editorial Team
Heads up: Always check admit cards only on the official website of the recruiting body. Beware of fake links on social media. The official portals are listed below.

How to check your admit card (step by step)

  1. Visit the official website of the recruiting body (links below) about 1–2 weeks before the exam.
  2. Open the 'Admit Card' or 'Download Call Letter' section.
  3. Log in using your registration number and password, or roll number and date of birth.
  4. Check every detail — name, photo, exam date, reporting time and centre — and report any error immediately.
  5. Download the admit card and take at least two clear colour printouts.
  6. Carry the printout plus an original photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Voter ID) to the centre.

What you'll need

  • Registration number & password
  • Roll number / date of birth
  • A printer for a hard copy
  • A valid original photo ID for exam day

Frequently asked questions

When is the admit card released?

Usually 1–2 weeks before the exam date. The exact date is announced on the official website and we list it as soon as it is out.

I forgot my registration number or password — what now?

Use the 'Forgot Registration No./Password' link on the official login page and recover it with your registered email or mobile number.

There is a mistake on my admit card — can I fix it?

Minor mismatches (like photo/signature) are sometimes correctable through the official correction window or by contacting the regional office. Act immediately and keep proof.

What must I carry to the exam centre?

A printed admit card and an original valid photo ID. Follow the exact dress and item rules printed on the admit card.
