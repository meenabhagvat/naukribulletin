📌 How to Download Admit Card

  1. Click "Download" link — you'll go to the official exam website
  2. Click "Download Admit Card / Hall Ticket" on the official site
  3. Enter Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password
  4. Download the PDF and take 2 printouts for the exam
  5. Carry a valid photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN) along with admit card
