How Age Relaxation Works

Government exam age limits have built-in relaxations for reserved categories. OBC candidates typically get 3 years of relaxation, SC/ST get 5 years, PwD candidates get 10 years (15 for SC/ST PwD), and Ex-Servicemen get relaxation equal to their service period plus 3 years. EWS candidates generally follow the General category age limit with no additional relaxation.

Age is calculated as of 1st January or 1st August of the exam year depending on the exam. Always verify the exact cutoff date from the official notification before applying.
