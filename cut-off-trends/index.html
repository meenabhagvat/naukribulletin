Frequently asked questions

What is the SSC CGL cut-off for 2024?

The SSC CGL 2024 Tier 1 cut-off for UR category is approximately 152 marks out of 200. OBC is around 143. Always verify on ssc.gov.in.

Why do cut-offs increase every year?

Cut-offs rise when applicant numbers increase, the paper is easier, or fewer vacancies are released.

Is there category-wise cut-off?

Yes. Every exam has separate cut-offs for UR, OBC, SC, ST and EWS as per reservation norms.
