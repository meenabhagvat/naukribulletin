What We Do

Every day, our automated system scans dozens of official government sources — PIB, FreeJobAlert, Sarkari Result, and more — extracting the latest job notifications, results, admit cards, and current affairs. Our AI then formats this raw government data into clean, easy-to-read job pages.

We cover all major government job categories: SSC, Railway (RRB), Banking (IBPS, SBI, RBI), UPSC, Defence (Army, Navy, Air Force), Police, Teaching, and State PSC jobs across all Indian states.

Our Promise

NaukriBulletin.in is completely free. No paid membership, no premium listings. We believe every aspirant deserves equal access to job information, regardless of their economic background.

We are funded by Google AdSense advertising — ads you see on the site help us keep the lights on and the scrapers running 24/7.
