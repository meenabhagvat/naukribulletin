About NaukriBulletin

Verified government job notifications, exam syllabi and preparation tools for Indian aspirants.

NaukriBulletin.in is an independent information site for people preparing for Indian government jobs. It is run from Satna, Madhya Pradesh by Ravi Prakash Namdeo.

What we publish

Recruitment notifications from central and state government bodies, PSUs, universities and research institutes. Exam syllabus pages, previous year papers, cut-off trends, an age and eligibility checker, a 7th Pay Commission salary calculator and free mock tests.

How a job page is made

Every job page is built from the official notification PDF or the recruiting body's own website. A page is published only when the notification states the last date, the official application link, and the vacancy, qualification, age and pay details. If any of these are missing, we wait for the official corrigendum instead of guessing.

We use software to track official websites for new notices and to lay out the facts in a standard format. The dates, numbers and links on each page come from the official notice, which is linked on that page. Once the last date passes, the page stays up as a record but is removed from search engines and our sitemap.

Corrections

If something on a page differs from the official notification, the official notification is correct. Email info@naukribulletin.in with the page link and we will fix it, usually within 24 hours.

How the site is funded

The site is free to use. It is supported by advertising from Google AdSense. Advertisers have no say in what we publish, and we never charge candidates for job information. We are not connected to any government department or recruitment board. See our Disclaimer, Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
