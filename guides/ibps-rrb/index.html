IBPS RRB 2026 at a glance

IBPS runs one common process for Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), also called Gramin Banks. The current cycle is CRP RRBs-XV. Registration ran from 1 to 21 September 2026. After the One State One RRB amalgamation in May 2025, the number of RRBs fell, and 28 RRBs are reported as taking part in this cycle.

Eligibility, age and fee

  • Education: a bachelor's degree in any discipline for both Office Assistant and Scale I. For Scale I, a degree in agriculture, IT, management, law, economics or accountancy is given preference. The result must be declared by 21 September 2026.
  • Age as on 1 September 2026: Office Assistant 18-28 years. Officer Scale I above 18 and below 30 years.
  • Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (non-creamy layer) 3 years, PwBD 10 years. See age relaxation rules.
  • Fee: Rs 850 for General, OBC and EWS. Rs 175 for SC, ST and PwBD (and ex-servicemen for Office Assistant).
  • Language: you must know the language the RRB prescribes. Studying it in Class 8 or above counts as proof. An RRB may give up to six months after joining to acquire it.

You can apply for Office Assistant and one officer post (Scale I, II or III). The state you choose for Office Assistant and Scale I cannot be changed.

Pattern differences: Office Assistant vs Scale I

The two Prelims look alike. The real differences are in the level of the Mains questions and the interview for Scale I. One-fourth of a mark is cut for each wrong answer.

  • Office Assistant: selection on Mains marks only. No interview.
  • Scale I: Mains, then a common interview. Final merit combines both.
  • Difficulty: the Scale I paper has harder puzzles and data interpretation, and General Awareness leans more on banking and financial topics.

Hindi or English: what you can choose

This is the biggest advantage of RRB exams for Hindi-medium candidates.

  • In Mains, the language section can be taken in either English or Hindi. You do not have to attempt an English language section at all.
  • The other sections are bilingual. You can read each question in English or Hindi.
  • The medium differs by state. For Bihar, Haryana and Rajasthan the paper is in English and Hindi. For Uttar Pradesh, Urdu is added. Some states add the regional language, such as Marathi in Maharashtra or Telugu in Telangana.

For a candidate who studied in Hindi up to Class 12, choosing Hindi for the 40-mark language section can add several marks compared with an English paper. Practise Hindi grammar, synonyms and passage questions from past RRB papers before you decide. The choice you make in the form is what appears in the exam.

The Scale I interview

  • The interview carries 100 marks.
  • Minimum qualifying marks: 40% for General and 35% for SC, ST, OBC and PwBD.
  • Final merit uses an 80:20 weightage: 80% from the Mains score and 20% from the interview.
  • It is a common interview, held after the Mains result, for all RRBs in the process.

Questions in RRB interviews stay close to rural banking. Prepare these: Kisan Credit Card, priority sector lending, self-help group linkage, crop loans and interest subvention, PM Jan Dhan, Mudra and the role of NABARD. Know the sponsor bank of your RRB, its head office and the main crops of your home district. Candidates who can explain why they want a rural posting, in simple words, do better than those who recite definitions.

Which RRB operates in Madhya Pradesh

Earlier MP had two RRBs: Madhya Pradesh Gramin Bank and Madhyanchal Gramin Bank. Under the One State One RRB policy, both were merged from 1 May 2025 into a single bank, Madhya Pradesh Gramin Bank, sponsored by Bank of India, with its head office in Indore. So in RRB XV, every MP vacancy is in this one bank.

Office Assistant or Scale I: which to prefer

  • Apply for both. The Prelims syllabus is almost the same, and IBPS lets you apply for Office Assistant plus one officer post.
  • If you are 28 or older on 1 September 2026, only Scale I is open to you in the General category.
  • Scale I pays about double the basic and leads to branch manager roles. It also means loan targets, recovery work and transfers within the RRB's area.
  • Office Assistant suits those who want a stable job close to home. Internal promotion exams to officer cadre exist in RRBs.

If you clear both, most candidates take Scale I. Pick Office Assistant if your family needs you near home and the RRB's officer postings are far.

Common mistakes and documents to keep ready

  • Wrong state: the state choice is final. Choose a state whose language you studied in Class 8 or above.
  • Two officer posts: applying for both Scale I and Scale II is not allowed. Only one officer post plus Office Assistant.
  • Degree date: the result must be out by 21 September 2026.
  • Category certificates: OBC non-creamy layer and EWS in the central format, valid for the year the notice states.
  • Language section: do not pick English by default if you are stronger in Hindi.

Keep ready: Class 8, 10 and 12 marksheets (for language and date of birth), degree and marksheets, caste or EWS certificate, PwBD certificate if any, photo ID, the printed form and the call letter. Track admit card release on the admit card page.

A 6-week plan for the Prelims

  1. Weeks 1-2: puzzles and seating arrangement daily, plus simplification and approximation. Prelims has only two sections, so speed decides the cut-off.
  2. Weeks 3-4: one full Prelims mock every day in 45 minutes, with 25 and 20 minute sectional limits. Note the sections where you run out of time.
  3. Weeks 5-6: start Mains topics: computer knowledge basics, rural banking awareness and your chosen language section. Keep one Prelims mock every alternate day.

For General Awareness in Mains, read news on RBI decisions, NABARD schemes, agriculture credit and MP state schemes from June 2026 onward. RRB papers ask more rural and agriculture questions than IBPS Clerk or SBI Clerk. Use our mock tests and previous year papers.

Frequently asked questions

How many vacancies are there in IBPS RRB 2026?

CRP RRBs-XV lists 13,706 indicative posts: 8,183 Office Assistant, 4,256 Officer Scale I, 1,047 Scale II and 220 Scale III.

Which RRB operates in Madhya Pradesh now?

Madhya Pradesh Gramin Bank, sponsored by Bank of India with its head office in Indore. It was formed on 1 May 2025 by merging the old Madhya Pradesh Gramin Bank and Madhyanchal Gramin Bank.

How many RRB posts are there in MP this year?

As reported from the notice, Madhya Pradesh Gramin Bank has 484 Office Assistant and 252 Officer Scale I vacancies. These are indicative and can change before allotment.

Can I write the IBPS RRB language section in Hindi?

Yes. In Mains you choose either English or Hindi for the language section. Other sections are bilingual.

Is there an interview for Office Assistant?

No. Office Assistant selection is on Mains marks only. Officer Scale I has a 100-mark interview with 80:20 weightage for Mains and interview.

What is the age limit for IBPS RRB 2026?

As on 1 September 2026, Office Assistant is 18-28 years and Officer Scale I is above 18 and below 30 years. Reserved categories get relaxation.

When is the IBPS RRB 2026 Prelims?

IBPS has given November or December 2026 as the tentative window. Exact dates were not announced as of 16 October 2026.
