SSC CHSL 2026 at a glance

SSC Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL) is the main central government exam for Class 12 pass candidates. It fills Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Data Entry Operator (DEO) and DEO Grade A posts in ministries, departments and offices across India.

The 2026 notice (Advertisement No. HQ-C1102/05/2026-C-1) was published on 7 September 2026 with about 2,536 tentative vacancies. The post-wise and category-wise split was not released with the notice. SSC says it will publish it later, and the final number can change.

Delhi postings fall in the X class. You are covered under the National Pension System. Use the salary calculator for a month-wise estimate, or read our guide to pay levels.

Eligibility

  • Age: 18-27 years as on 1 August 2026, so born between 2 August 1999 and 1 August 2008. OBC get 3 years, SC/ST 5 years and PwBD 10 years of relaxation. Check yours on the age calculator.
  • Education: Class 12 pass from a recognised board by the cut-off date in the notice.
  • DEO in the office of the Comptroller and Auditor General (C&AG): Class 12 in the Science stream with Mathematics is compulsory. Arts and Commerce students cannot be allotted these posts.
  • Fee: ₹100. Women, SC, ST, PwBD and eligible ex-servicemen are exempt.

If you are unsure about your marksheet or category, run it through the eligibility checker.

Tier 1 pattern

Tier 1 is a 60 minute objective computer based test. Each question is worth 2 marks and 0.50 marks are cut for every wrong answer. Tier 1 is qualifying: it decides who goes to Tier 2, but its marks are not added to the final merit.

Scribe-eligible candidates get 80 minutes. The minimum qualifying marks are 30% for UR, 25% for OBC and EWS, and 20% for others, but the actual shortlist cut-off is decided by vacancies and is far higher.

Tier 2 pattern

Tier 2 has two sessions on the same day. Session I is an objective test. Every question in Session I carries 3 marks and 1 mark is cut for a wrong answer.

Final merit is made from Sections I and II, a total of 360 marks. Computer Knowledge and the skill or typing test are qualifying only, but you cannot get a post without clearing them. Full topic lists are on our syllabus page.

Typing test and DEO skill test

  • LDC/JSA typing test, English: 35 words per minute (about 10,500 key depressions per hour), 10 minutes.
  • LDC/JSA typing test, Hindi: 30 words per minute (about 9,000 key depressions per hour), 10 minutes.
  • DEO in most departments: 8,000 key depressions per hour on the computer, 15 minutes, on a passage of about 2,000-2,200 key depressions.
  • DEO in specified departments: 15,000 key depressions per hour, 15 minutes.

You choose Hindi or English typing in the form, and you cannot switch at the test. Many Hindi medium candidates from MP pick Hindi typing because they type Hindi at work, but the test software may not use the keyboard layout they are used to. Read the skill test instructions SSC issues with the admit card and practise on the layout it names. Errors are calculated to 2 decimal places, so a few extra mistakes in a long passage can fail you.

How marks are actually decided

  1. Normalisation: Tier 1 runs in many shifts over about a month. SSC normalises scores across shifts using its notified method. Your normalised score, not the raw score, is compared with the cut-off.
  2. Tier 1 shortlist: category-wise cut-offs are fixed so that enough candidates reach Tier 2 for each post type.
  3. Tier 2 merit: the final list is made from Session I Sections I and II, out of 360 marks. Computer Knowledge must be passed but does not add marks.
  4. Skill test: only candidates in the zone of selection take it. Failing it removes you from that post.
  5. Post and department allotment: by merit and the post and department preferences you give. Pick preferences carefully since they are binding.

Because Tier 2 has 1 mark negative for 3 marks positive, blind guessing hurts less than in Tier 1, but a wrong answer still costs a third of a correct one. Attempt a question only if you can rule out two options.

Which post to prefer and why

DEO pays more (Level 4 or 5 against Level 2 for LDC). The work is mostly data entry, and some DEO posts in specified departments need 15,000 key depressions per hour. If you can type fast and accurately, DEO gives a higher start.

LDC/JSA starts at Level 2 but the work is general office work: files, noting, dak and records. Promotions in Central Secretariat and attached offices go through UDC and Assistant grades, and departmental exams can speed this up. Many CHSL clerks also prepare for SSC CGL while in service, since a government job gives stable income during preparation.

If you are a Science-Maths student, keep the C&AG DEO posts in your preferences: fewer candidates are eligible for them.

A 45-day Tier 1 plan and mistakes to avoid

With Tier 1 starting on 30 November 2026, you have about six weeks from today.

  1. Week 1-2: one full mock test to find weak sections. Then daily: 1 hour arithmetic (percentage, ratio, profit and loss, time and work, averages), 45 minutes reasoning, 45 minutes English grammar and vocabulary.
  2. Week 3-4: add algebra, geometry and trigonometry basics. Revise static GK (polity, history, geography) and read current affairs of the last 6 months. One mock every two days.
  3. Week 5-6: one full mock daily in 60 minutes, at the time of your expected shift. Spend more time reviewing wrong answers than taking the test. Solve previous year papers of CHSL 2023-2025.
  4. Every day: 20 minutes of typing in your chosen language, so the skill test is not a last-minute problem.

Common mistakes: spending too long on one maths question, guessing in Tier 1 where each wrong answer costs a quarter of a correct one, using a photo or signature in the correction window that does not match your ID, and choosing Hindi typing without practising the test keyboard.

Documents to keep ready

Document verification happens after Tier 2 and the skill test, at the user department or the SSC regional office. Candidates lose posts at this stage for small, avoidable reasons. Keep these ready well in advance:

  • Class 10 certificate (proof of date of birth) and Class 12 marksheet and pass certificate.
  • OBC (non-creamy layer) or EWS certificate in the central government format, issued for the right financial year. A state-format OBC certificate from MP is usually not accepted for central posts.
  • SC/ST certificate, PwBD certificate or ex-servicemen discharge papers, if you claimed them.
  • Photo ID with the same name and date of birth as your application.
  • No Objection Certificate from your employer if you already work in government.

If your name is spelled differently on your marksheet and Aadhaar, get a gazette notification or affidavit done now. It takes weeks, and SSC does not give extra time at verification. Our guide on how to read a government job notification explains which clauses decide these cases.

Frequently asked questions

When is SSC CHSL 2026 Tier 1 exam?

SSC has listed Tier 1 tentatively from 30 November to 31 December 2026. Exact shift dates come with the city slip and admit card.

What is the age limit for SSC CHSL 2026?

18-27 years as on 1 August 2026, with 3 years relaxation for OBC, 5 years for SC/ST and 10 years for PwBD.

Do Tier 1 marks count in the final merit?

No. Tier 1 is qualifying. Final merit is based on Tier 2 Sections I and II, out of 360 marks.

What typing speed is needed for LDC in CHSL?

35 words per minute in English or 30 words per minute in Hindi, in a 10 minute test. It is qualifying.

What is the salary of LDC and DEO through CHSL?

LDC/JSA is at Pay Level 2 (₹19,900-63,200). DEO is at Level 4 (₹25,500-81,100) or Level 5 (₹29,200-92,300), and DEO Grade A at Level 4.

Can a Commerce student become DEO in C&AG?

No. DEO posts in C&AG need Class 12 in Science with Mathematics. Commerce and Arts students can apply for other DEO and LDC posts.
