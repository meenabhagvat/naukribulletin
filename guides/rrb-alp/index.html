Exam overview and current status

Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) is the entry post for train drivers in Indian Railways. The latest notice is CEN 01/2026 for 11,127 posts across all RRBs, at Pay Level 2 with initial pay Rs 19,900.

Applications ran from 15 May to 14 June 2026, with fee payment till 16 June and a paid modification window from 17 to 26 June. As reported in September 2026, CBT-1 is tentatively scheduled for 3-5 November 2026. The city slip usually comes about 10 days before your exam and the e-call letter about 4 days before. Confirm on your RRB website and our admit card page.

Selection: CBT-1, CBT-2 (Part A and Part B), CBAT (the psycho test), then Document Verification and Medical Examination at standard A-1.

Eligibility: age and ITI or diploma trades

Age: 18-30 years as on 1 July 2026. Relaxation is 3 years for OBC (NCL) and 5 years for SC/ST, with separate rules for ex-servicemen and serving staff. ALP is not open to PwBD candidates, because of the medical standard.

RouteQualification
ITIClass 10 plus ITI (NCVT/SCVT), or Course Completed Act Apprenticeship, in Fitter, Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Millwright/Maintenance Mechanic, Mechanic (Radio and TV), Electronics Mechanic, Mechanic (Motor Vehicle), Wireman, Tractor Mechanic, Armature and Coil Winder, Mechanic (Diesel), Heat Engine, Turner, Machinist, or Refrigeration and AC Mechanic
DiplomaClass 10 plus 3-year diploma in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Automobile Engineering (or a combination)
DegreeDegree in the same engineering branches, accepted in place of the diploma

The qualification must be complete by 14 June 2026. Results awaited is not accepted. Trades like COPA, Welder or Draughtsman are not in the list, so those ITI holders cannot apply. Fee was Rs 500 (Rs 400 refunded after CBT-1) or Rs 250 for SC, ST, ex-servicemen, women, transgender, minorities and EBC (refunded after CBT-1).

Negative marking is one third of a mark per wrong answer in CBT-1 and both parts of CBT-2. CBAT has none. CBT-1 under CEN 01/2026 has no General Awareness section, which earlier ALP notices had.

Minimum marks in CBT-1 and Part A: 40% UR and EWS, 30% OBC (NCL) and SC, 25% ST. Basic Science and Engineering covers engineering drawing, units and measurement, basic electricity, occupational safety and similar topics.

Part B for diploma and degree holders: you pick one trade from your branch group. Electrical branch: Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Wireman, Armature and Coil Winder or Refrigeration and AC Mechanic. Mechanical: Fitter, Mechanic (Motor Vehicle), Turner, Machinist, Mechanic (Diesel) and others. Pick the trade whose ITI syllabus you already know best. For many candidates that is Electrician on the electrical side and Fitter on the mechanical side.

How marks are actually decided

  • CBT-1 to CBT-2: 15 times the vacancies of your RRB are called, by normalised CBT-1 marks. CBT-1 marks are then thrown away.
  • CBT-2 to CBAT: 8 times the community-wise vacancies, by Part A marks, but only if you clear 35% in Part B.
  • CBAT: you must get a T-score of at least 42 in each of the five batteries separately. One weak test ends your chance, whatever your Part A score.
  • Final merit: 50% Part A marks plus 50% CBAT score, RRB-wise. Ties go to the older candidate, then alphabetical order.
  • Normalisation applies to CBT-1, CBT-2 and any multi-shift test.

The practical lesson: Part A and CBAT are worth the same. Do not leave CBAT practice for the last week. After CBT-1, split your time closer to evenly between Part A and CBAT.

CBAT psycho test

CBAT is a computer based aptitude test designed under RDSO guidelines. It is held in English and Hindi only. The five batteries widely reported from past cycles are Memory, Following Directions, Depth Perception, Concentration and Perceptual Speed.

  • Each battery is timed. Speed with accuracy matters, and there is no negative marking, so do not leave answers blank.
  • The T-score compares you with others who took the test. Practising the formats on a computer, with a mouse and keypad, is what moves it.
  • On CBAT day you must submit the Self-Screening Medical Declaration (Annexures VIIIA and VIIIB) from the notice.

Medical standard A-1

A-1 is the strictest railway medical standard. Check it with an eye doctor before you spend a year preparing.

  • Distant vision 6/6 in each eye without glasses, with a fogging test (must not accept +2D).
  • Near vision Sn 0.6 in each eye without glasses.
  • Must pass colour vision, binocular vision, field of vision, night vision and mesopic vision tests.
  • Candidates who had LASIK or any refractive eye surgery are not eligible.

Before CBT-2, ask a government eye hospital or a qualified ophthalmologist to check three things: unaided 6/6 in each eye, the fogging test, and colour vision on a colour plate test. If any one fails, a strong CBT score cannot save the selection, because the railway medical is done after DV and is final.

If you wear even mild glasses, ALP is closed to you. Look at RRB Group D posts with B-1 or C-1 standards, which allow glasses.

Salary and running allowance

Basic pay is Rs 19,900 at Pay Level 2 (range Rs 19,900-63,200). DA is 60% from 1 January 2026. During training only a stipend is paid.

Running allowance is what makes ALP pay higher than other Level 2 jobs. It is paid as Kilometreage Allowance (KMA) for distance actually run, or Allowance in lieu of Kilometreage (ALK) for duty without running. Under Railway Board RBE 85/2019, an ALP got Rs 391 per 100 km (Rs 251 for shunting) and Rs 602 ALK per 160 km. These rates are revised from time to time, so check the latest RBE. As an illustration at the 2019 rate, 4,000 km in a month would be about Rs 15,640 extra.

Also, 30% of basic pay is treated as the pay element of running allowance for certain benefits. See pay levels explained and the salary calculator.

RRB choice and common mistakes

  • One RRB, one application. A second application gets both rejected and can lead to debarment. The RRB cannot be changed after submission.
  • You rank zones only inside your RRB. Once empanelled for one zone you lose the lower preferences. Under CEN 01/2026 the largest shares were in East Coast, South Eastern, Central and Northeast Frontier Railway.
  • Shortlists for CBT-2 and CBAT are RRB-wise, so an RRB with more posts calls more people at each stage.
  • Choosing a Part B trade you studied least because it looks easy. It is a 35% qualifier, so pick the one you know.
  • Wearing glasses or having had LASIK and applying anyway. The medical is the last stage, so this wastes the whole cycle.
  • Photo with glasses or cap. The notice bars both in the live photo.

A 90-day plan after CBT-1

  1. Days 1-30: Part B trade theory from the DGT syllabus of your trade, one unit a day. Basic electricity and engineering drawing for Part A.
  2. Days 31-60: Part A maths and reasoning sets of 100 questions in 90 minutes, three times a week. Start CBAT practice tests every second day.
  3. Days 61-90: Full CBT-2 mocks in the 90 + 60 minute format. Daily CBAT drills for memory and depth perception. Get an eye test done against A-1 and keep the vision certificate ready. Use our mock tests and previous year papers.

Keep ready for DV: Class 10 certificate, ITI or diploma marksheets and certificate, NCVT/SCVT or apprenticeship certificate, caste or EWS certificate, and Aadhaar. Read how to read a notification before the next ALP CEN.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for RRB ALP with an ITI in COPA or Welder?

No. CEN 01/2026 accepts only the listed trades such as Fitter, Electrician, Mechanic (Diesel), Wireman and Turner, or a diploma or degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Automobile Engineering.

Can I become an ALP if I wear glasses?

No. Medical standard A-1 needs 6/6 distant vision in each eye without glasses, and candidates who had LASIK are not eligible.

Do CBT-1 marks count in the final merit?

No. CBT-1 is only for shortlisting to CBT-2. Final merit is 50% CBT-2 Part A and 50% CBAT.

What is the qualifying mark in CBAT?

A T-score of at least 42 in each test battery, for all categories. There is no negative marking in CBAT.

When is RRB ALP CBT-1 for CEN 01/2026?

Reports in September 2026 gave 3-5 November 2026 as the tentative CBT-1 dates. Confirm on your RRB website.

What is the salary of an Assistant Loco Pilot?

Basic pay is Rs 19,900 at Level 2. With 60% DA and HRA it is about Rs 33,800-37,800 before running allowance, which is paid extra for kilometres run.
