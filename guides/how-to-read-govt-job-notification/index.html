Why the notification PDF matters more than any summary

An official recruitment notification is the legal document for that recruitment. If a coaching post, a WhatsApp forward or a news summary says something different, the notice wins. Most rejected forms come from candidates who read a summary and missed one line in the PDF: a qualification cut-off date, a post that needs a specific subject, or a certificate format.

Notices from SSC, RRB and MPESB run from 20 to over 100 pages. You do not need to read every word, but you do need to know where the parts that decide your eligibility sit. This page walks through them in the order they usually appear, using real notices from 2025.

MPESB is the one that trips people up. It publishes a common rule book for the exam and a separate document listing posts by department. You must read both, because qualification and post-specific conditions are often only in the second one.

Step 1: Check the first page and the source

Before reading anything else, confirm three things on page one:

  1. Notice number and date. SSC uses a file number and date (the CGL 2025 notice was released on 9 June 2025). RRB uses a Centralised Employment Notice number such as CEN 08/2024 for Group D. MPESB uses an exam name and year with a rule book.
  2. Where you downloaded it from. Only ssc.gov.in, the regional RRB websites (for example rrbbhopal.gov.in), esb.mp.gov.in, mppsc.mp.gov.in or another .gov.in or .nic.in domain. A PDF from a Telegram channel may be edited.
  3. Key dates box. Opening date, last date to apply, last date for fee, correction window and tentative exam date. For SSC CGL 2025 the last date was 4 July 2025, with fee till 5 July and correction from 9-11 July.

Step 2: Read the vacancy table and category split

The vacancy table shows each post, its Pay Level, the department, and the number of posts by category: UR, EWS, OBC, SC, ST, and within those, PwBD and ESM. Look for these details:

  • Tentative or final. SSC and RRB vacancies are usually marked tentative and can change before results.
  • Zone or state wise split. RRB Group D CEN 08/2024 had 32,438 posts divided by railway zone. Your chance depends on the zone you choose, and you can apply to only one RRB.
  • PwBD suitability column. It tells which disability categories each post accepts.
  • Backlog posts. MPESB notices often show backlog vacancies separately. In MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 3 (2025), of 454 posts the split was UR 146, EWS 29, SC 58, ST 69, OBC 152.

Step 3: Eligibility: age, essential qualification and the crucial date

This section decides whether you can apply at all. Read it slowly.

Age

Look for the "age as on" date and the birth date range. SSC CGL 2025 counted age on 01.08.2025. RRB Group D CEN 08/2024 counted age as on 01.01.2025 with a limit of 18-36 years. MPESB 2025 notices used 01.01.2025. Category relaxations are listed in a table here; the age relaxation guide explains them.

Essential vs desirable qualification

Essential qualifications are compulsory. Desirable ones only help in some selections and cannot make you eligible on their own. Watch for post-specific conditions. In CGL 2025, Junior Statistical Officer needed 60% in Class 12 Mathematics or Statistics as a subject in graduation. RRB Group D accepted Class 10, ITI from an NCVT/SCVT institute or a National Apprenticeship Certificate.

Qualification cut-off date

Most notices say you must hold the qualification on or before a stated date, usually the closing date or the crucial date. A final-year student whose result comes after that date is ineligible even if they pass. Check whether "result declared" or "appearing" candidates are allowed.

In the exam pattern section, note the number of questions, negative marking, sectional timing and whether marks are normalised across shifts. In the selection section, find out which stages are qualifying only and which add to merit. For RRB Group D the PET is qualifying and merit comes from the CBT.

Fee is paid only through the payment gateway on the official portal. No genuine recruiter asks for fee by personal UPI ID, phone number or bank transfer to an individual.

Step 5: The document and certificate formats annexure

The last 10-20 pages usually hold certificate formats: OBC-NCL, EWS, PwBD, ESM undertaking, departmental NOC. These are not decoration. If your certificate is not in the given format or not dated within the window, it can be rejected at document verification, months after the exam.

  • Note the issue-date window for OBC-NCL and EWS certificates.
  • Check photo and signature specifications. SSC now takes a live photo during the online application, and a mismatch with the exam day photo causes trouble.
  • For MP posts, keep the mool niwas certificate and caste certificate in the MP format. The MPESB rule book (niyam pustika) lists them.

Spotting fake notices, websites and payment scams

PIB Fact Check regularly flags fake recruitment notices. Real examples:

  • Fake RPF notice, February 2024. A circular claiming 4,660 RPF Sub-Inspector and Constable posts, with fees and dates, spread on social media. PIB said the Ministry of Railways had not issued it. RRB later published its own official CENs. The lesson: wait for the notice on the official RRB website, even if the numbers look right.
  • "Rashtriya Unnati Kendra" website, December 2021. It claimed links with the Skill Development Ministry and asked for Rs 1,645 as application fee for government project jobs. PIB said the ministry had no connection with it.
  • General warning, August 2026. PIB Fact Check cautioned job seekers against fake job notices, emails and messages that ask for registration, medical or document verification fees, promise guaranteed jobs, or ask for OTP, Aadhaar or bank details. A government logo on a letter does not prove it is real.

Red flags

  • Domain is not .gov.in or .nic.in, or is a lookalike (extra words, hyphens, .org, .in without gov).
  • "Direct joining", "no exam" or "selection guaranteed" for a Group C post.
  • Fee requested to a personal UPI or wallet, or a refundable "security deposit".
  • Appointment letter on WhatsApp without any exam or interview.

Report suspicious notices to PIB Fact Check at factcheck@pib.gov.in or WhatsApp +91 8799711259. NaukriBulletin lists only notices linked to official websites on the jobs page.

Checklist before you press submit

  1. Notice downloaded from the official website, and notice number noted.
  2. Age checked on the crucial date with relaxation. Use the age calculator or the eligibility checker.
  3. Essential qualification held by the cut-off date, including any subject or percentage condition.
  4. Category certificate in the right format and date window.
  5. Name, father's name and date of birth exactly as in Class 10 certificate.
  6. Post preferences or zone chosen after checking vacancies and Pay Level.
  7. Exam centre choices made, and the city you can actually travel to.
  8. Fee paid through the portal, with transaction ID and a PDF of the submitted form saved.
  9. Correction window dates noted in case you find an error.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I download the official recruitment notification?

Only from the recruiter's own site, such as ssc.gov.in, your regional RRB website, esb.mp.gov.in or mppsc.mp.gov.in. Treat PDFs shared on social media as unverified until you find them there.

What is the difference between essential and desirable qualification?

Essential qualification is compulsory to apply. Desirable qualification may give preference but cannot make you eligible if you lack the essential one.

What is the crucial date in a notification?

It is the date on which your age, and often your qualification, is checked. For SSC CGL 2025 it was 01.08.2025; for RRB Group D CEN 08/2024 age was counted as on 01.01.2025.

How do I know if a job notice is fake?

Check that it appears on the official .gov.in or .nic.in website. Fee asked to a personal UPI, guaranteed selection, or appointment without exam are signs of a scam.

Can I apply if my final-year result is pending?

Only if the notice allows it. Most notices require the qualification to be held by a stated cut-off date, and candidates without the result by then are ineligible.

Where do I report a fake recruitment notice?

Send it to PIB Fact Check at factcheck@pib.gov.in or WhatsApp +91 8799711259, and to the cyber crime helpline 1930 if you lost money.
