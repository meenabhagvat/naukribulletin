Exam overview and current status

RRB Group D is the common name for recruitment to Level 1 posts in Indian Railways. The latest notice is CEN 09/2025, published on 31 January 2026 by all Railway Recruitment Boards together. It lists 22,195 provisional vacancies at Pay Level 1.

Online applications ran from 31 January to 2 March 2026, with fee payment till 4 March. Corrigendum 1 dated 7 February 2026 revised the vacancy table and let applicants change their chosen RRB, post and zone preferences without a modification fee. The CBT was held in August 2026 and the provisional answer key came out on 7 September 2026. As of mid-October 2026 the result and PET dates had not been officially announced. Watch your own RRB website and our results page.

Selection has four stages: CBT, Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Age and eligibility

  • Age: 18-33 years as on 1 January 2026. Upper age relaxation: OBC (NCL) 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, UR and EWS PwBD 10 years. Ex-servicemen, serving railway staff, widowed or divorced women and course completed act apprentices have separate rules.
  • Education: Class 10, or ITI, or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC), as specified post-wise in Annexure A. It must be held by 2 March 2026. Candidates awaiting results were not eligible.
  • Name and date of birth must match the Class 10 certificate, or the ITI/NAC certificate for those applying on that basis.
  • Fee: Rs 500 for general candidates, of which Rs 400 is refunded after appearing in the CBT. Rs 250 for SC, ST, ex-servicemen, PwBD, women, transgender, minorities and EBC, fully refunded after appearing. Refunds go only to an Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

Check your age against the cut-off with the age calculator and read age relaxation rules.

CBT pattern

There is a single CBT. No second stage written exam.

Time is 90 minutes (120 minutes for scribe users). One third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer. Minimum qualifying marks: 40% for UR and EWS, 30% for OBC (NCL), SC and ST. PwBD candidates may get up to 2 marks relaxation if short of vacancies.

Science is Class 10 level physics, chemistry and biology. It carries as much weight as maths, and many candidates under-prepare it.

How marks are actually decided

  • Normalisation: the CBT runs in many shifts across weeks, so RRBs normalise raw marks before making lists. Your normalised score can be a few marks above or below your answer-key count.
  • PET shortlist: about 3 times the community-wise vacancies of each RRB, by CBT merit.
  • DV and medical shortlist: 1:1 with vacancies, from those who pass PET, again by CBT merit.
  • PET adds no marks. Your rank is fixed by the CBT. PET only keeps you in or puts you out.
  • Negative marking maths: a wrong answer costs 0.33. If you can cut four options to two, a guess has positive expected value. A blind guess does not.
  • Course completed act apprentices get one third weightage for NCVT marks. The higher of their CBT score or weighted score is used.

Checking your own score: open the response sheet from the answer key link, count right and wrong answers per section, then compute right minus one third of wrong. Do this before the objection window closes, because a successful objection on a wrong key can change your count. Do not compare your raw score with a friend from another shift. Shift difficulty is exactly what normalisation adjusts for, and the cut-off is applied to the normalised figure, RRB-wise and community-wise.

PET standards

TestMaleFemale
Weight carry35 kg for 100 m in 2 minutes, one go20 kg for 100 m in 2 minutes, one go
Run1,000 m in 4 min 15 sec1,000 m in 5 min 40 sec

Weight carry comes first. If you put the weight down or fail it, you are out and do not run. PwBD candidates, course completed act apprentices and ex-servicemen are exempt from PET. Pregnant candidates found so at PET are kept in abeyance and tested six weeks after delivery.

Men need a pace of about 15 seconds per 100 m. Practise the full 1 km on a measured track, not on a road with an app that drifts. Practise the weight carry with a sack of the exact weight.

Salary

All posts are at Pay Level 1, initial basic pay Rs 18,000 (range Rs 18,000-56,900). DA is 60% of basic from 1 January 2026. HRA is 30%, 20% or 10% of basic by city class.

RRB and post choice strategy

You can apply to only one RRB. You cannot change it after the modification window, and transfer requests are not registered until 5 to 10 years of service. So the choice matters more than most people think.

  1. Look at Annexure B for your category, not the total. An RRB with many UR posts may have very few ST posts.
  2. Think about where you will work for a decade. For Madhya Pradesh candidates, RRB Bhopal is the home board; check which zones and units it lists in Annexure B. Choosing a far RRB for a lower cut-off can mean years away from home.
  3. Rank every post you are eligible for and medically fit for. A post you leave out is treated as unwillingness. If you wear glasses above 4D, do not rank A-2, A-3 or B-1 posts at the top.
  4. Put the post you want first. Once allotted, you lose the claim to every other post, even ones lower on your list.

Common mistakes that get forms or candidates rejected

  • Filling more than one application. All applications are rejected and the candidate can be debarred.
  • Photo with cap, mask or glasses, or a name that differs from the Class 10 certificate.
  • Bank account not seeded with Aadhaar, so the fee refund fails.
  • OBC (NCL) or EWS certificate not valid on the cut-off date of 2 March 2026.
  • Not practising the 35 kg carry. It eliminates candidates before the run.
  • Hiding a past LASIK surgery. It surfaces at the medical, after months of waiting.

Documents and a 60-day plan

Keep ready: Class 10 certificate, ITI or NAC certificate if applying on that basis, caste or EWS certificate in central format, PwBD or ex-servicemen papers if claimed, Aadhaar, and the self-declaration for no LASIK if you are placed in an A-2 or A-3 post.

  1. Days 1-20: Class 10 science chapters and arithmetic basics, 25 questions of each daily.
  2. Days 21-40: Reasoning (series, coding, direction, syllogism) and six months of railway and national current affairs. Start a 1 km run three times a week.
  3. Days 41-60: One full 100-question mock test every day in 90 minutes, plus previous year papers. Add the weight carry twice a week.

Frequently asked questions

What is the age limit for RRB Group D under CEN 09/2025?

18-33 years as on 1 January 2026, with 3 years relaxation for OBC (NCL), 5 years for SC/ST and 10 years for UR/EWS PwBD.

Is there negative marking in RRB Group D?

Yes. One third of a mark is deducted for every wrong answer in the 100-question CBT.

What is the PET for RRB Group D?

Men carry 35 kg for 100 m in 2 minutes and run 1,000 m in 4 min 15 sec. Women carry 20 kg for 100 m in 2 minutes and run 1,000 m in 5 min 40 sec.

Do PET marks count in the merit list?

No. PET is qualifying only. The merit list is based on normalised CBT marks.

Can I wear glasses and still join Group D?

Yes, for A-3, B-1 and C-1 posts within the allowed lens power. A-2 posts such as Pointsman-B need 6/9 vision in each eye without glasses.

When will the RRB Group D 2026 result come?

The CBT was held in August 2026 and the provisional answer key came out on 7 September 2026. The result date had not been announced as of mid-October 2026.
