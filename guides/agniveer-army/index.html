Agniveer under Agnipath: the basics

Since 2022, all soldier-level (below officer rank) entry into the Indian Army is through the Agnipath scheme. Selected candidates join as Agniveers for a fixed term of 4 years, including training. At the end of 4 years, up to 25 percent of each batch can be selected for the regular Army. The rest leave with a lump sum called Seva Nidhi.

Recruitment is run by the Army through its Army Recruiting Offices (AROs). Registration, admit cards and results are on joinindianarmy.nic.in. Selection has two phases: an online Common Entrance Exam (CEE) first, then a recruitment rally with physical and medical tests.

What changed this year: the upper age limit was raised to 22 years. Candidates born between 1 July 2005 and 1 July 2009 were eligible, which means 17.5 to 22 years. Earlier cycles had an upper limit of 21. Candidates could also apply for more than one category if they met the rules for each. Past cycles opened registration in February, so expect the next notice around early 2027, but wait for the official notice.

Chest for men is 77 cm with 5 cm expansion. Height and chest are lower for some regions and communities (for example hill regions and North East) as listed in the ARO notification. Women can join as Agniveer GD (Women Military Police) through a separate notice.

Which category to choose: GD has the most vacancies and the lowest education bar, so competition is highest. If you have Class 12 PCM with good marks, Technical has fewer applicants and leads to trades with skills useful after 4 years. Clerk/SKT needs 60 percent in Class 12 and a lower height, so it suits candidates who are shorter than 170 cm. Tradesman (cook, washerman, tailor and similar) is for candidates with Class 8 or 10 only.

Common Entrance Exam (CEE) pattern

The CEE is a computer-based test held in 13 languages. Results are normalised because the test runs over many days and shifts.

For GD, each question is worth 2 marks and 25 percent of the marks are deducted for each wrong answer, so a wrong answer costs 0.5 marks. Unattempted questions get zero. Published pass marks are 35 for GD and 80 for Technical; the cut-off for being called to the rally is usually higher and differs by ARO, because candidates are shortlisted against the vacancies of each ARO.

Physical Fitness Test: how marks are scored

Candidates who clear the CEE are called to the rally. For men in GD, the Physical Fitness Test (PFT) carries marks.

1.6 km run, Group IUp to 5 min 30 sec60

Pull-ups (beam)10 / 9 / 8 / 7 / 6 reps40 / 33 / 27 / 21 / 16

Above 5 min 45 sec in the run, you are out. Fewer than 6 pull-ups, you are out. Extra time is given at high-altitude rally sites. For women, the 1.6 km run is 7 min 30 sec for Group I and 8 minutes for Group II, with a 10 feet long jump and 3 feet high jump to qualify.

How the final merit works: merit is prepared ARO-wise from CEE marks, PFT marks and bonus marks, among those who pass the measurement and medical tests. Bonus marks are given for NCC certificates, ITI or diploma, sports achievements, and for sons of serving soldiers, ex-servicemen and war widows. The 60 marks for a fast run can decide your rank, so train for under 5 min 30 sec, not just to pass.

Over 4 years, the Agniveer puts in Rs 5.02 lakh and the government matches it with Rs 5.02 lakh. On exit, the Seva Nidhi is about Rs 10.04 lakh plus interest earned, and it is exempt from income tax. There is no gratuity or pension for the 4-year term. Agniveers get a non-contributory life insurance cover of Rs 48 lakh during service, along with risk and hardship, dress and travel allowances as applicable. Food, accommodation and medical care are provided, so most of the in-hand amount can be saved.

25 percent retention and life after 4 years

At the end of 4 years, Agniveers can volunteer for permanent enrolment. Up to 25 percent of each batch is selected for the regular cadre, based on performance during the 4 years and the needs of the Army. Selection is centralised and transparent as per the scheme rules. Those selected serve a further full term as regular soldiers, with pension rules applicable to regular service.

For the 75 percent who leave, the Ministry of Home Affairs has announced reservation and age relaxation for ex-Agniveers in Central Armed Police Forces and Assam Rifles, and several states including Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh have announced reservation in state police and other jobs. Check the current percentage in each recruitment notice, since these rules have been revised more than once. See also SSC GD Constable, where ex-Agniveers get relaxation.

Common mistakes at registration and rally

  • Wrong ARO. You apply under the ARO of your domicile district. Applying elsewhere with a different address leads to rejection at document checks.
  • Marks below the category rule. Many Clerk applicants have 60 percent aggregate but less than 50 percent in English. Check every subject against the rule.
  • Weak pull-ups. Candidates train only for running. Pull-ups carry 40 marks. Train on a proper beam with full arm extension, because half reps are not counted.
  • Medical issues found late. Ear wax, flat feet, knock knees, colour blindness and dental gaps are common reasons for being declared unfit. Get a basic check before the rally.
  • Missing documents. Carry original Class 10 certificate, domicile, caste and character certificates (in the format given by the ARO), NCC and sports certificates for bonus marks, and an unmarried certificate.

A 90-day plan for the next cycle

  1. Days 1-30: Run 3 km every morning at an easy pace. Start pull-ups with negative reps. Study Class 9-10 Maths and Science for 2 hours daily.
  2. Days 31-60: Add 1.6 km time trials twice a week. Target 6 minutes, then 5 min 45 sec. Add GK and reasoning. Solve 2 sectional tests a day.
  3. Days 61-90: Full CEE mocks on a computer, 50 questions in 60 minutes. Push the run under 5 min 30 sec and reach 10 pull-ups.

Frequently asked questions

What is the age limit for Army Agniveer?

For the 2027 recruitment cycle notified in February 2026, the age limit is 17.5 to 22 years, for candidates born between 1 July 2005 and 1 July 2009.

How much does an Agniveer earn?

The monthly package is Rs 30,000 in the first year rising to Rs 40,000 in the fourth year. 30 percent goes to the Seva Nidhi corpus and the government adds an equal amount.

How much is Seva Nidhi after 4 years?

About Rs 10.04 lakh plus interest, made up of the Agniveer's own contribution and an equal government contribution. It is exempt from income tax.

Is there negative marking in Agniveer CEE?

Yes. One quarter of the marks for a question are deducted for every wrong answer.

What is the 1.6 km running time for Agniveer GD?

Up to 5 min 30 sec earns 60 marks and 5 min 31 sec to 5 min 45 sec earns 48 marks. Slower than 5 min 45 sec is a fail.

How many Agniveers become permanent?

Up to 25 percent of each batch can be selected for the regular Army after 4 years, based on performance and vacancies.

When will the next Agniveer notification come?

It has not been announced as of October 2026. Recent cycles opened registration in February, but check joinindianarmy.nic.in for the official notice.
