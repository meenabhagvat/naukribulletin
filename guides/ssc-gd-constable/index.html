SSC GD 2026 at a glance

SSC GD Constable is the largest regular central recruitment open to Class 10 pass candidates. Through one exam, the Staff Selection Commission fills Constable (General Duty) posts in BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB and SSF, and Rifleman (General Duty) posts in Assam Rifles. The notice for the 2026 cycle came out on 1 December 2025 with 25,487 vacancies: 23,467 for men and 2,020 for women.

The 2026 computer based exam (CBE) was first set to start on 23 February 2026, was then postponed, and was finally held from 27 April to 30 May 2026. About 21.57 lakh candidates sat the exam. The result came out on 12 October 2026 and 1,97,408 candidates were shortlisted for the physical tests.

CAPF and Assam Rifles posts are split state-wise and district-wise. Some posts are kept for border districts and Naxal or militancy affected districts. So you compete only with candidates of your own state, gender and category. SSF is filled on an all-India basis.

Age and education

Age: 18-23 years as on 1 January 2026. That means you must be born between 2 January 2003 and 1 January 2008. Use the age calculator if you are close to either end.

  • OBC: 3 years relaxation (up to 26)
  • SC/ST: 5 years relaxation (up to 28)
  • Ex-servicemen: 3 years after deducting military service
  • Children of 1984 riot or 2002 Gujarat riot victims: 5 years (UR), 8 years (OBC), 10 years (SC/ST)

Education: Class 10 (Matriculation) pass from a recognised board, with the result declared on or before 1 January 2026. A result still awaited on that date does not count.

Fee: ₹100. Women, SC, ST and eligible ex-servicemen do not pay a fee.

CBE pattern

The CBE is one objective paper of 60 minutes. Each question carries 2 marks and 0.25 marks are cut for every wrong answer.

The paper is set in English, Hindi and 13 regional languages: Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu. Part D is only English or Hindi. Most candidates from MP choose Hindi for Part D because it is the easier scoring option for them. How marks are actually decided

Your raw score is not your final score. The 2026 exam ran in many shifts over five weeks, so SSC normalises scores using the method in its notice of 2 June 2025. The scorecard shows both raw and normalised marks. Cut-offs are applied on normalised marks, which is why the cut-offs have long decimals, such as 126.83604.

  1. Qualifying marks: 30% for UR (48 of 160), 25% for OBC and EWS (40), 20% for SC, ST and ex-servicemen (32). This only makes you eligible. The real cut-off is much higher.
  2. NCC bonus: NCC C certificate holders get 5% of maximum marks (8 marks), B gets 3% (4.8) and A gets 2% (3.2). You must claim it in the form. It is added to the normalised score and checked at document verification. Ex-servicemen do not get it.
  3. Shortlisting: about 8 times the vacancies are called for PET/PST, and about 2 times for the medical and document stage.
  4. State-wise cut-off: merit is drawn separately by state, gender and category. In 2026, Haryana female UR cut-off was 128.42 and Rajasthan male UR was 126.84. UP and Bihar male UR crossed 120.
  5. Tie-break: higher marks in Part A, then Part B, then Part C, then the older candidate, then alphabetical order of name.

PET is qualifying only. It does not add marks. Final merit is the CBE normalised score plus any NCC bonus.

Height relaxation is given only if you bring the certificate in the prescribed format. If you disagree with a height or chest reading, you can appeal on the same day through the presiding officer. There is no appeal against the race result. Weight should be proportionate to height and age.

Medical examination

After PET/PST, shortlisted candidates go through the Detailed Medical Examination (DME). It follows the MHA Uniform Guidelines for medical examination (2015, revised by the MHA order of 31 May 2021), which are linked in the notice. If declared unfit, you can ask for a Review Medical Examination as per the notice.

From what candidates commonly face at DME, the frequent reasons for rejection are weak eyesight, colour blindness, knock knees, flat feet, varicose veins, ear wax or hearing problems, and dental issues. Get a basic eye test and a check of knees and feet done before PET. Some of these can be treated in a few weeks. Earlier SSC GD notices listed minimum uncorrected distant vision of 6/6 in the better eye and 6/9 in the other. Confirm the current standard in the 2026 guidelines.

Salary and force allocation

All posts in this exam are at Pay Level 3: ₹21,700-69,100. On top of basic pay you get DA at the current central rate, HRA based on your posting city (or free government housing), transport allowance, and in many postings risk or hardship allowances. CAPF jawans also get ration money, uniform allowance and access to CAPF canteens. Try the salary calculator for a monthly estimate, and see our guide on government pay levels.

How the force is decided: you ranked all seven forces in the application form, and that order cannot be changed now. SSC goes down the merit list. Each candidate gets the highest preference that still has a vacancy in their state and category. A candidate with a high score gets the first choice. A candidate near the cut-off usually gets whatever is left, which in 2026 will mostly be CISF because it has the most posts.

Which force to prefer: CISF guards airports, metros, ports and industrial units, so postings are mostly in cities with fixed duty hours. BSF and ITBP mean long border tours, often in hard terrain. CRPF has the most movement, including internal security duty. Assam Rifles is mostly in the North-East. Think about family, health and how far from home you can live before you rank, because a later change is not allowed.

What to do now, and a plan for the next cycle

If you qualified the 2026 CBE: PET/PST dates are not out yet. Start running daily. The 5 km in 24 minutes target means a pace under 4 min 48 sec per km. Build up to it over 6-8 weeks. Do not try it for the first time on test day.

Documents to keep ready: admit card, Class 10 certificate, photo ID, caste certificate in central format, domicile or PRC for the state and district you entered in the form, NCC certificate if claimed, height relaxation certificate if claimed, and ex-servicemen papers if any. A domicile certificate that does not match the state or district in your form leads to cancellation.

If you are preparing for the 2027 cycle (90 days):

  1. Days 1-30: one hour of Maths (percentage, ratio, profit and loss, time and work, simple interest) and one hour of Reasoning daily. Read Class 9-10 NCERT for history, geography and polity. Run 3 km every morning.
  2. Days 31-60: one mock test every two days. Read current affairs of the last 8 months. Push running to 5 km.
  3. Days 61-90: daily full mock in 60 minutes. Note every wrong answer. Time a full 5 km run every week.

Common mistakes that cost candidates: giving a different state or district from the domicile certificate, forgetting to tick NCC in the form, guessing blindly when every wrong answer costs 0.25 marks, and arriving at PET untrained.

Frequently asked questions

Is SSC GD 2026 result out?

Yes. SSC declared the CBE result on 12 October 2026. A total of 1,97,408 candidates were shortlisted for PET/PST.

What is the age limit for SSC GD 2026?

18-23 years as on 1 January 2026, so you must be born between 2 January 2003 and 1 January 2008. OBC get 3 years and SC/ST get 5 years of relaxation.

Is there negative marking in SSC GD?

Yes. Each question carries 2 marks and 0.25 marks are deducted for every wrong answer.

What is the race for SSC GD?

Men must run 5 km in 24 minutes and women 1.6 km in 8.5 minutes. In Ladakh it is 1.6 km in 7 minutes for men and 800 m in 5 minutes for women.

What is the salary of SSC GD Constable?

All posts are at Pay Level 3, ₹21,700-69,100 basic pay, plus DA, HRA or housing, transport allowance and other allowances as per posting.

Can I change my force preference after the result?

No. The notice says preferences given in the application cannot be changed later. Forces are allotted by merit and that preference order.

Does PET add to my marks?

No. PET and PST are qualifying. Final merit is based on the normalised CBE score plus any NCC bonus marks.
