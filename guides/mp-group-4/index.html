What MPESB Group 4 is

Group 4 is the combined recruitment test that the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB, earlier Vyapam or PEB) holds for clerical and typing posts in state departments. The main posts are Assistant Grade 3 (sahayak grade 3), Steno-typist, Stenographer, Data Entry Operator, and smaller posts such as Coding Clerk, Record Clerk, Receptionist-cum-Clerk and Store Keeper. Each post in each department has its own post code in the rule book.

The exam is held roughly every one to two years. The 2023 cycle had 3,047 posts. The latest completed cycle, called the Group 4 Combined Recruitment Test 2024, was advertised in early 2025 with 966 posts.

Reservation and age relaxation apply only to MP domicile candidates. A candidate from Uttar Pradesh or Rajasthan can apply, but only as UR and with the UR age limit. Check your age with the age calculator once the new cut-off date is announced.

CPCT: the condition that decides most forms

CPCT (Computer Proficiency Certification Test) is conducted under the MP Department of Science and Technology. It has two parts: an objective computer awareness paper and a typing test in English and Hindi. The score card shows your typing speed in each language. For Assistant Grade 3 and Data Entry posts, the rule book asks for this score card with at least 20 wpm in Hindi and/or 30 wpm in English, depending on the post.

  • CPCT is held only a few times a year and the result takes weeks. If you do not have a valid score card yet, register for the next CPCT session now. Do not wait for the Group 4 notice.
  • Check the validity date printed on your score card. A score card that has expired before the date fixed in the rule book will not be accepted.
  • Practise Hindi typing on the same keyboard layout you will select in the CPCT test. Many candidates who type fast on mobile fail the 20 wpm Hindi bar on a keyboard.
  • If your post needs Hindi and you have only English speed, or the reverse, your form can be rejected at document verification even after you clear the exam.

Exam pattern

The written exam is an online test of 100 objective questions for 100 marks. Each correct answer gets 1 mark and there is no negative marking. In the latest cycle the exam ran in two shifts, 9-11 am and 3-5 pm. Duration and the subject split can change, so confirm them in the new rule book. The split reported for the latest cycle was:

SubjectMarks

Since there is no negative marking, attempt every question. A blank answer is a lost chance.

Syllabus in brief

  • General Knowledge: MP geography, rivers, districts, tribes, history and culture, MP government schemes, Indian polity, economy, science basics and current affairs. MP questions are a large share, so do not study only national GK.
  • General Hindi: sandhi, samas, synonyms (paryayvachi), antonyms, muhavare and lokoktiyan, shuddh vartani, and comprehension.
  • General English: tenses, articles, prepositions, synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, error spotting and comprehension at Class 10 level.
  • Mathematics: percentage, ratio, average, profit and loss, simple and compound interest, time and work, time and distance, and basic mensuration.
  • Computer: MS Word, Excel and PowerPoint, shortcut keys, internet, email, file formats, basic hardware and cyber safety.
  • Aptitude and Reasoning: series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, analogy and simple puzzles.

See the syllabus page for the detailed topic list and solve previous year papers of 2023 and 2025 to see the MP GK level.

Salary and probation

Assistant Grade 3 posts in MP are usually at Level 4 of the state pay matrix, ₹19,500-62,000. Stenographer and some other posts are at higher levels. The pay for each post code is printed in the rule book. You also get DA at the state rate, HRA as per posting city and other state allowances. See our guide to pay levels and the salary calculator.

Probation pay: since 2019, MP paid new Class 3 and Class 4 recruits 70%, 80% and 90% of the minimum of the pay scale in the first three years of probation. In September 2026 the Madhya Pradesh High Court struck down this scheme and directed full salary during probation. Whether the state appeals, and how the order is applied to new appointees, was not clear as of mid-October 2026. Ask your department for the current position at joining.

Selection and post preference

  1. Online exam: merit is drawn post code-wise and category-wise from your marks. The exam is held in many shifts, so MPESB normalises marks across shifts.
  2. Post choice: you fill preferences of post codes in the form. A higher rank gets the first preference that still has a seat. Rank posts by department and district you actually want. Changing a joining later is not easy.
  3. Skill test: where the rule book requires one, mainly for stenography posts, it is held by the department or as the rule book states.
  4. Document verification: done by the hiring department, not MPESB. This is where CPCT, computer diploma and domicile papers are checked.

Which post to prefer: Assistant Grade 3 in district offices (Collectorate, Tehsil, Zila Panchayat) means desk work close to home, with promotion to Grade 2 and Grade 1 over the years. Directorate posts in Bhopal have more workload but better exposure. If you have shorthand, Stenographer posts start at a higher level, and fewer candidates meet the shorthand condition.

Common mistakes that get forms rejected

  • Applying without a CPCT score card, or with one that has expired or does not show the required language speed.
  • Showing a computer course that is not from a recognised institution, or a course of less than one year.
  • Claiming reservation without an MP caste certificate issued by a competent authority in MP.
  • Entering a date of birth that does not match the Class 10 certificate.
  • Not checking post-specific conditions, such as shorthand speed or a degree for a few post codes.
  • Missing the correction window and finding the mistake only at document verification.

Documents to keep ready: Class 10 and 12 marksheets, CPCT score card, computer diploma certificate, MP domicile certificate, caste certificate, EWS certificate for the current year if claimed, and Aadhaar. Our guide on reading a job notification explains how to read the post table.

A 90-day plan

  1. Days 1-30: register for CPCT if needed and start 30 minutes of daily typing in both Hindi and English. Cover arithmetic chapters and Hindi grammar. Read MP GK from one standard book.
  2. Days 31-60: finish English grammar, computer basics and reasoning. Revise MP GK every week. Take one mock test every three days.
  3. Days 61-90: daily full mock of 100 questions. Revise MP schemes and current affairs of the last 8 months. Keep typing practice going so your CPCT and skill test speed stays up.

If you are also preparing for MP Patwari, most of the GK, Hindi, maths and computer portions overlap.

Frequently asked questions

Is CPCT compulsory for MP Group 4?

For Assistant Grade 3 and data entry type posts, yes. The latest rule book asked for a CPCT pass with 20 wpm Hindi and/or 30 wpm English typing, as the post requires.

When is the MPESB Group 4 2026 exam?

The MPESB calendar of January 2026 listed it tentatively for September 2026. As of 16 October 2026 we could not confirm a released rule book or fixed exam date, so check esb.mp.gov.in.

What is the age limit for MP Group 4?

In the latest cycle it was 18-40 years as on 1 January 2024. MP-domicile SC, ST, OBC candidates, women and government servants could apply up to 45 years.

Is there negative marking in MPESB Group 4?

No. There are 100 questions of 1 mark each and no marks are cut for wrong answers.

Can candidates from other states apply for MP Group 4?

Yes, but only in the unreserved category with the general age limit. Reservation and age relaxation are for MP domicile candidates.

What is the salary of Assistant Grade 3 in MP?

Assistant Grade 3 is usually at Level 4 of the MP pay matrix, ₹19,500-62,000, plus DA and HRA. Check the pay listed against your post code in the rule book.
