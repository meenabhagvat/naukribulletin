Age Relaxation Rules 2026: OBC, SC/ST, PwBD, ESM and MP Rules

How age relaxation works

Every recruitment notice sets a minimum and maximum age, for example 18-27 years. Age relaxation raises only the upper limit for certain categories. It never lowers the minimum age. A candidate who is 17 years and 11 months on the crucial date is ineligible whatever the category.

For central government posts the rules come from the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), and SSC, RRB and other central recruiters reproduce them in a table in each notice. State governments make their own rules. Madhya Pradesh uses a different system based on domicile, explained further below.

Before you apply, check your exact age on the crucial date with the age calculator, then add the relaxation you are entitled to.

Central government relaxation by category

These are the standard relaxations used in SSC notices (category codes 01-18) and followed in most central recruitment.

The departmental and widow limits are absolute ages, not years added. A General category widow can apply up to 35 even if the post limit is 27. The exact upper ages for departmental candidates differ by exam, so read the table in that year's notice.

Ex-servicemen, PwBD and other special cases

Ex-servicemen

The ESM rule is a calculation. Take your actual age on the closing date, subtract the length of military service, and the result must not exceed the upper age limit by more than 3 years. Example: a 38-year-old ex-serviceman with 17 years of service has an effective age of 21. For a post with an upper limit of 27 he is eligible, because 21 is within 27 + 3. ESM who have already joined a civil post in Group C or D on regular basis using ESM concession generally cannot use ESM status again for another such post. Check the ESM clause in the notice.

Persons with Benchmark Disability

PwBD relaxation applies only if you have a disability certificate showing at least 40% benchmark disability, and only for posts identified as suitable for your disability category. The notice lists which categories (VH, HH, OH, others) each post can take. A PwBD candidate applying for a post not identified for their disability gets no relaxation.

Former Agniveers

For constable posts in Central Armed Police Forces, the Ministry of Home Affairs has given former Agniveers age relaxation (5 years for the first batch, 3 years for later batches). This appears in the SSC GD notice. See the SSC GD Constable guide.

The crucial date: the day your age is counted on

Age is always measured on one fixed date printed in the notice, often called the crucial date or "age as on" date. Your age on the day you fill the form does not matter.

RecruiterUsual crucial dateRecent example
SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS)1 August or 1 January of the exam yearCGL 2025: 01.08.2025
RRB1 January or 1 JulyGroup D CEN 08/2024: 01.01.2025
MPESB / MPPSC1 January of the yearMPESB Group 2 Sub Group 3 (2025): 01.01.2025

Read the birth date range in the notice rather than doing the maths yourself. SSC CGL 2025, for an 18-27 post, required birth between 02.08.1998 and 01.08.2007. A candidate born on 01.08.1998 is one day too old. Add your relaxation to the earliest permitted year, not to your age on the form date.

Only the date of birth recorded in your Class 10 certificate is accepted. Affidavits, Aadhaar or a later corrected certificate are not used to change it.

How Madhya Pradesh rules differ

MP does not add years category by category in the central way. It sets a fixed upper limit based on whether you are an MP domicile (mool niwasi) and which group you belong to. The General Administration Department (GAD) circular of 13 January 2016, as amended, sets the base table. Recent MPESB and MPPSC notices apply it like this for non-uniformed posts:

MP domicile candidateUpper age limit
Unreserved men40 years
EWS40 years
SC, ST, OBC (men and women)45 years
Women of all categories, including unreserved45 years
MP government, corporation, board employees; Home Guard (Nagar Sainik)45 years
PwD45 years
  • Women get relaxation in MP even if unreserved. This has no central equivalent.
  • Reservation and relaxation are for MP domiciles only. A Rajasthan SC candidate applying in MP is treated as unreserved and does not get the 45-year limit. The GAD 2016 table caps non-domicile candidates lower, so read the non-domicile line in each notice.
  • Uniformed posts have lower limits. MPPSC lists 33 years for uniformed posts in State Service, with category relaxation on top. MP Police constable notices set their own limits.
  • One-time extensions happen. MP has at times given an extra temporary relaxation for a recruitment year. These apply only to the period stated in the order.

For MP Patwari and other MPESB group exams, see the MP Patwari guide.

Can you combine two relaxations?

Generally no. You take the single relaxation that gives you the highest limit. The main exceptions are written into the table itself: PwBD stacks with OBC (13 years) and with SC/ST (15 years), and widow or departmental limits are higher for SC/ST. An OBC ex-serviceman cannot add 3 years for OBC on top of the ESM calculation unless the notice says so. If two rows apply to you, choose the better one in the form and keep proof for that category only.

Three eligibility checks worked out

  1. OBC candidate, SSC CHSL-type post (18-27), crucial date 1 January 2026. The OBC limit becomes 30. A candidate born on 15 March 1996 is 29 years 9 months on 01.01.2026, so eligible. A candidate born on 20 December 1995 has crossed 30 and is out.
  2. MP domicile unreserved woman, MPESB post, crucial date 1 January 2026. Her upper limit is 45, not 40, because women of all categories get the higher limit in MP. If she applies for an SSC post the same year, her limit drops back to the general one.
  3. SC candidate with 45% locomotor disability, central post (18-27). PwBD + SC gives 15 years, so the limit is 42, provided the post is identified as suitable for OH candidates in the notice.

In each case the check is done on the crucial date only. A birthday falling one day after that date does not make you overage.

Common errors that get forms rejected

  1. Claiming OBC with a creamy layer or old certificate. Central posts need an OBC-NCL certificate in the central format, issued within the window stated in the notice. A state OBC certificate does not work if your caste is not on the central OBC list.
  2. Claiming EWS relaxation. EWS gets reservation but no age relaxation in central jobs.
  3. Using MP domicile rules for a central exam. Women do not get extra age for SSC or RRB posts.
  4. Wrong crucial date. Candidates often count age on the last date of application. The notice may use 1 January or 1 August instead.
  5. PwBD certificate below 40% or for an unsuitable post. Rejection happens at document verification, after you have cleared the exam.
  6. ESM without discharge proof. Serving personnel within one year of release need the undertaking and certificate format given in the notice.
  7. Selecting a category you cannot prove. Once a category is claimed in the form, it usually cannot be changed after the correction window closes.

Documents to keep ready

  • Class 10 certificate or marksheet showing date of birth
  • Caste certificate (SC/ST) or OBC-NCL certificate in the central format for central posts, MP format for MP posts
  • MP domicile certificate (mool niwas praman patra) for MP posts
  • Disability certificate (UDID or medical board) with percentage
  • Discharge book or service certificate for ex-servicemen
  • Death certificate of husband or divorce decree, plus a non-remarriage declaration, for the widow/divorced category
  • Office certificate of continuous service for departmental candidates

Frequently asked questions

How many years of age relaxation does OBC get in central jobs?

3 years for OBC non-creamy layer. With PwBD it rises to 13 years.

Do women get age relaxation in SSC or Railway exams?

Not as a general category. Only widows, divorced or judicially separated women who have not remarried get a higher limit, up to 35 years (40 for SC/ST) in SSC notices.

What is the upper age limit for MP government jobs?

For MP domicile candidates in non-uniformed posts, recent MPESB and MPPSC notices use 40 years for unreserved men and EWS, and 45 years for SC, ST, OBC, women, PwD and MP government employees. Check each notice.

Does EWS get age relaxation?

No. EWS gets reservation in central and MP posts but no extra age.

Which date is my age counted on?

The crucial date printed in the notice, such as 01.08.2025 for SSC CGL 2025 or 01.01.2025 for RRB Group D CEN 08/2024. Your age on the form date does not matter.

Can a candidate from another state get MP reserved category relaxation?

No. In MP, reservation and the higher age limits are for MP domicile candidates only. Others are treated as unreserved.
