Latest status: 2026 cycle

Patwari recruitment in Madhya Pradesh is run by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), the body earlier called Vyapam and later PEB. Patwari posts come under the Revenue Department and are filled through the Group-2 Sub-Group-4 Combined Recruitment Test.

On 4 August 2026 MPESB opened applications for the Group-2 Sub-Group-4 Combined Recruitment Test 2026. Media reports put the combined test at about 2,106 posts across several departments, such as Panchayat Coordination Officer, Assistant Sub Inspector and Data Entry Operator. The Patwari share in this cycle is a special drive of 200 posts reserved for persons with disabilities (PwD): 94 for hearing impairment, 45 for locomotor disability and 61 for multiple disability, autism, intellectual disability or mental illness.

If you are not a PwD candidate, there is no open general Patwari recruitment as of October 2026. A new general cycle has not been announced. Keep checking the exam calendar and the MPESB site.

What happened in the 2023 exam

The last large general Patwari exam was the Group-2 Sub-Group-4 and Patwari Recruitment Test 2022, held online from 15 March to 26 April 2023 in two shifts a day. About 12 lakh candidates appeared, as per Amar Ujala's result report.

The result came on 30 June 2023. Allegations followed because several toppers had written the exam at one centre in Gwalior. The state government put appointments on hold in July 2023 and asked retired High Court judge Justice Rajendra Kumar Verma to inquire. The committee gave the exam a clean chit in early 2024 and district counselling for selected candidates started on 24 February 2024.

For you, the lesson is practical: a Patwari result can take months to turn into a joining letter. Keep your documents, domicile and caste certificates valid during that gap, and do not drop other exams while you wait.

Eligibility: graduation and CPCT

  • Education: graduation in any subject from a recognised university. The final degree must be complete by the last date of the form. Final-year students cannot apply.
  • CPCT: a Computer Proficiency Certification Test scorecard covering computer proficiency and Hindi typing. The 2026 notice, as reported, allows a selected candidate who lacks CPCT to pass it within three years of appointment, that is, within the probation period. If you skip it, you risk your job confirmation.
  • Domicile: reservation and fee concession are only for MP natives. Candidates from other states apply as unreserved.
  • Employment registration: a live registration on the MP Rojgar portal is mandatory before you apply.
  • 2026 special drive only: a valid disability certificate for the category you apply under.

Exam pattern

The Patwari paper is an online objective test of 200 marks with two parts of 100 marks. In 2023 there were 200 questions of 1 mark each, 3 hours, in Hindi and English, with no negative marking. The 2026 notice keeps the same two-part, 200-mark structure, as per reports.

Syllabus, topic by topic

Part A

  • General Science and GK: physics, chemistry and biology up to Class 10, MP geography, rivers, districts, tribes, history and current affairs of the state and country.
  • General Hindi: sandhi, samas, muhavare, paryayvachi, vilom, vakya shuddhi, comprehension.
  • General English: grammar, vocabulary, error spotting, fill in the blanks, passages.
  • General Mathematics: percentage, ratio, profit and loss, simple and compound interest, time and work, mensuration, average.

Part B

  • Computer: MS Office, internet, email, shortcut keys, basic hardware and software, cyber safety.
  • Reasoning: series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction, syllogism, non-verbal figures.
  • General Management: basics of planning, organising, leadership, communication and decision making.
  • GK and Aptitude: static GK, schemes of the MP government, land revenue basics.

Salary and probation pay

The MPESB rule book lists the Patwari scale as Rs 5,200-20,200 with Grade Pay Rs 2,100, revised under the MP 7th Pay Commission matrix. DA and HRA are added at the rates the state notifies.

Selection, posting and training

  1. Online exam: merit is prepared on the exam score. Reports on the 2026 cycle mention a minimum qualifying percentage; check the rule book for your category.
  2. District allotment: selected candidates fill district preferences and are allotted a district by merit.
  3. Document verification and counselling: done at the allotted district, as in February 2024.
  4. Medical and appointment: the Collector issues the appointment order under the Revenue Department.

After joining, a Patwari is trained in land records work: khasra, B-1, naksha, mutation (namantaran), demarcation (seemankan) and the state's online land records systems. The length and place of training follow the Commissioner Land Records orders for your batch. Practical work begins in a halka of a few villages under the Revenue Inspector and Tehsildar.

Common mistakes that get forms rejected

  • Applying as a final-year student. The degree result must be out by the last date.
  • Leaving the Rojgar registration expired. Renew it before the form, not after.
  • Claiming MP reservation with a caste certificate from another state.
  • Wrong disability category in the 2026 drive. The post codes are split by disability type, so pick the one on your certificate.
  • Name or date of birth that does not match the Class 10 marksheet and Aadhaar. Aadhaar-based verification is used at the centre.
  • Uploading a blurred photo, signature or the handwriting sample that the form asks for.
  • Assuming CPCT is optional. The relaxation only delays it; it does not remove it.
  • Not saving the application number. The admit card login needs it.

Documents to keep ready for counselling: graduation marksheets and degree, Class 10 marksheet for date of birth, MP domicile certificate, caste or EWS certificate valid for the year, disability certificate if applicable, CPCT scorecard if you have it, Rojgar registration card, Aadhaar and four recent photographs.

A 90-day plan

Days 1-30: finish Class 6-10 science and maths basics, one Hindi grammar book and one English grammar book. Start a daily 20-minute Hindi typing session, which also prepares you for CPCT.

Days 31-60: MP GK (districts, rivers, tribes, dams, schemes), computer fundamentals and reasoning. Solve one 2023 shift paper every three days and note weak topics.

Days 61-90: full 200-question mocks in 3 hours, at the same time as your shift. Since there is no negative marking, practise finishing all questions with 10 minutes to spare. Use our mock tests and revise current affairs of the last 12 months.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a new MP Patwari vacancy in 2026?

Yes, but only as a special drive of 200 posts for persons with disabilities under the Group-2 Sub-Group-4 test 2026. No general Patwari recruitment has been announced as of October 2026.

Is CPCT compulsory for MP Patwari?

Yes. The notice asks for a CPCT scorecard with Hindi typing, but a selected candidate without it may pass CPCT within three years of appointment.

Can final-year students apply?

No. You must hold a completed graduation degree by the last date of the application.

Is there negative marking in the Patwari exam?

No negative marking was applied in the 2023 exam. Each question carried one mark.

What is the age limit for MP Patwari?

18 to 40 years for unreserved men and up to 45 years for MP reserved categories, women and PwD, counted as on 1 January of the recruitment year.

Who conducts the MP Patwari exam?

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), formerly Vyapam and PEB, at esb.mp.gov.in.
