UP Police Constable: the current cycle

Constable recruitment in Uttar Pradesh is run by the Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB). Its website is uppbpb.gov.in. The current cycle is Direct Recruitment 2025 for 32,679 posts. The online form ran from 31 December 2025 to 30 January 2026.

As of 16 October 2026, the written exam is over, the written result is out, and the physical stages have been held. The final selection list has not been declared yet.

Civil Police posts by category: UR 4,191, EWS 1,046, OBC 2,826, SC 2,198, ST 208. Women get 20 percent horizontal reservation in Civil Police as per UP rules.

Which post to prefer: Civil Police means posting in police stations across districts, close to the public, and it is the usual first choice. PAC is a battalion job with company-level deployment and frequent movement for law and order and elections. It has the most seats, so it is often easier to get. Jail Warder is under the Prisons department with a more fixed routine. All are at the same pay level, so choose based on the life you want.

Age and education

Education: Intermediate (Class 12) pass from a recognised board. Candidates still appearing in Class 12 were not eligible for this cycle.

Age as on 1 July 2025: The notified limit was 18-22 years for men and 18-25 years for women. On 5 January 2026 the UP government gave a one-time relaxation of 3 years to all categories for this recruitment. That makes the general upper limit 25 years for men and 28 years for women. SC, ST and OBC candidates who are domicile of UP get a further 5 years over this, as per state rules.

The 3-year relaxation was for the 2025 cycle only. Do not assume it will carry over to the next notice. Use our age calculator with the cut-off date of the notice you are applying under, and see age relaxation rules for how category relaxations add up.

The exam is offline on OMR, 2 hours. Each correct answer gives 2 marks and each wrong answer costs 0.5 marks (one quarter). The minimum qualifying score is 30 percent for UR, 25 percent for EWS and OBC, and 20 percent for SC and ST, but that is only a floor. The real cut-off is far higher.

Normalisation: The paper ran in six shifts over three days, so UPPRPB normalises scores across shifts. That is why the 2026 cut-offs were published with decimals, from 258.03661 for UR to 211.47988 for women ST. Your normalised score, not your raw score, is used for merit.

Merit: The board called 76,184 candidates, about 2.33 times the vacancies, to the physical stages. PST and PET are qualify-only. In the UP system, the final merit list is made from written exam marks of candidates who pass all physical stages, document checks and medical. So every mark in the written paper counts directly toward selection.

PET is pass or fail. There are no marks for finishing faster. Still, 25 minutes for 4.8 km is a steady pace of about 5 minutes 12 seconds per km, which untrained candidates fail. Start running 6 weeks before PST, not after the written result.

Measurement is done at PST before the run. A candidate who is 1 cm short is out, and the board allows an appeal only as per the notice. Measure yourself at a police line or a gym with a proper stadiometer before the PST date.

Salary and allowances

A UP Police Constable is at Pay Level 3 of the 7th Pay Commission matrix, with a pay range of Rs 21,700 to Rs 69,100. The same pay applies to Civil Police, PAC and Jail Warder.

  • Basic pay at joining: Rs 21,700
  • Dearness Allowance (DA) at the current state rate on basic pay
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA) based on the city of posting, or government quarters
  • Other allowances such as uniform, ration/messing and special duty allowances as per state orders

Deductions include NPS contribution and group insurance. Your in-hand pay will be higher than the basic because of DA and HRA. Use our salary calculator with the current DA rate, and read pay levels explained for how increments work.

What happened in the 2024 exam (60,244 posts)

The previous cycle was for 60,244 Constable Civil Police posts. About 48 lakh candidates applied. The first exam in February 2024 was cancelled after a paper leak. The re-exam was held on 23, 24, 25, 30 and 31 August 2024 in two shifts a day. The written result came on 21 November 2024, PST and PET followed in December 2024 to February 2025, and the final result was declared on 13 March 2025.

Two lessons from that cycle. First, the timeline from exam to joining took about seven months, so plan your finances and other exams around it. Second, the board took strict steps after the leak: biometric checks, CCTV and frisking. Reach the centre early with the exact documents asked for.

Documents to keep ready for DV

  1. Admit card and printout of the online application form
  2. High School (Class 10) certificate for date of birth
  3. Intermediate (Class 12) marksheet and certificate
  4. UP caste certificate (SC/ST/OBC) in the format given in the notice; OBC certificate must be recent for non-creamy layer
  5. EWS certificate for the relevant financial year
  6. UP domicile certificate
  7. Aadhaar card and one more photo ID such as voter ID or PAN
  8. Recent passport-size photos, same as the one uploaded
  9. Certificates for special categories: ex-serviceman, dependent of freedom fighter, sportsperson, home guard, if claimed

Common reasons for rejection: OBC certificate in central format instead of UP format, name spelling different from Class 10 certificate, and claiming a category in the form without a valid certificate. Fix these before the DV date, not at the venue.

Keep two sets of self-attested photocopies and the originals in a plastic folder. Physical stages are often held outdoors in hot weather, and a damaged or wet certificate creates avoidable trouble. If any certificate is in process, carry the application receipt, but know that the board can refuse a claim that is not backed by a valid certificate issued before the cut-off date in the notice.

Preparing for the next notice

The next UP Police Constable notice has not been announced as of October 2026. Use the gap well.

  • General Hindi (74 marks) is the highest-scoring part for UP candidates. Practise sandhi, samas, muhavare, paryayvachi and passages daily.
  • GK has a strong UP section: districts, schemes, history and culture of UP. Add 6 months of current affairs.
  • Maths and Reasoning: Class 10 level arithmetic. Speed matters because of negative marking.
  • Run three days a week from now. Use previous year papers from 2024 and 2026.

Also compare with SSC GD Constable and MP Police Constable if you want more than one attempt in a year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the status of UP Police Constable 2025-26?

The written exam was held on 8-10 June 2026 and the result came on 31 July 2026 with 76,184 candidates qualified. DV and PST began on 17 August and PET was held from 1 October 2026. The final result is awaited.

What is the age limit for UP Police Constable?

The notified limit was 18-22 years for men and 18-25 for women as on 1 July 2025. A one-time 3-year relaxation for all categories raised it to 25 and 28 for this cycle, with extra relaxation for UP reserved categories.

Is there negative marking in UP Police Constable exam?

Yes. Each correct answer gives 2 marks and 0.5 marks are deducted for each wrong answer.

How much running is required in UP Police PET?

Men must run 4.8 km in 25 minutes and women 2.4 km in 14 minutes. PET is qualifying only.

What is the salary of a UP Police Constable?

Pay Level 3, Rs 21,700-69,100, plus DA, HRA and other state allowances.

Do PET marks count in the final merit?

No. PST and PET are qualifying. The final merit is based on normalised written exam marks of candidates who clear all stages.
