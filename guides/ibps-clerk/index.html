IBPS Clerk 2026 at a glance

IBPS runs a Common Recruitment Process (CRP) every year for Customer Service Associate (CSA) posts, the clerical cadre in public sector banks. The current cycle is CRP CSA-XVI, for vacancies of the year 2027-28. A short notice came on 27 July 2026 and the full notification on 1 August 2026.

The indicative vacancy count is 11,403 across 11 banks: Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Bank of Maharashtra, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India, Indian Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, Punjab National Bank, Punjab & Sind Bank, UCO Bank and Union Bank of India. IBPS calls these numbers indicative, so they can change before allotment.

Selection has two written stages, Prelims and Mains. There is no interview. After Mains, IBPS makes a provisional allotment to a bank. The bank then runs document verification and, where needed, the local language test.

Eligibility, age and fee

  • Education: a graduation degree in any discipline from a recognised university, held on the closing date of registration.
  • Computer skills: operating and working knowledge of computers is compulsory. A certificate or a subject studied at school or college is accepted as proof.
  • Age: 20-28 years as on 1 August 2026, which means born between 2 August 1998 and 1 August 2006, both dates included.
  • Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (non-creamy layer) 3 years, PwBD 10 years over the category limit, ex-servicemen as per rules. See age relaxation rules.
  • Language: you must be able to read, write, speak and understand the official language of the state you apply for.
  • Fee: Rs 850 for General, OBC and EWS. Rs 175 for SC, ST and PwBD.

You can apply for vacancies of one state or UT only. This choice cannot be changed later, so decide it before you start the form. Use the age calculator with the 1 August 2026 cut-off date if you are close to the upper limit.

How marks are actually decided

  • Prelims marks are thrown away after shortlisting. They only decide whether you reach Mains.
  • Two cut-offs apply at Prelims: a minimum in each of the three sections and an overall cut-off. Both are fixed state-wise and category-wise. A high total with a weak English score can still fail.
  • Shift equating: Prelims runs in many shifts over two days. IBPS equates scores across shifts, so the marks on your scorecard can differ from the raw count you made from the answer key.
  • Mains decides everything: merit is drawn state-wise and category-wise on Mains marks out of 200, after you clear the sectional minimums there as well.
  • Negative marking hurts more in Reasoning, where questions carry 1.5 marks each in the reported pattern, so one wrong guess costs more there.

In practice, candidates from Hindi-belt states lose most often in the English section. Work on reading comprehension and error spotting early instead of leaving English for the last month.

Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT)

The LLPT is held by the allotted bank, after the Mains and provisional allotment but before joining. It is qualifying in nature.

  • Exempt: candidates who produce a Class 10 or higher marksheet or certificate showing they studied the opted local language.
  • Must take it: candidates who cannot show this at document verification.
  • If you fail: you are not offered appointment and drop out of the process.

This is how Canara Bank applied the rule for the last cycle. Its notice dated 24 June 2026 for CSA-XV listed candidates who had to take the LLPT, based on documents checked at verification held on 15, 16, 18 and 19 May and 8 June 2026. Details of the test were sent only to the registered email ID, so keep that inbox active.

For an MP candidate applying in Madhya Pradesh, a Class 10 marksheet with Hindi as a subject is usually enough. Carry the original and a self-attested copy to verification.

How provisional allotment works

After the Mains result, IBPS allots candidates to banks on merit-cum-preference. The inputs are your Mains score, your state, your category, the vacancies each bank reported for that state, and the bank preference order you gave in the form.

  • If your rank is high, you get your first or second bank. Lower ranks get whichever bank still has a seat.
  • Candidates who qualify but miss allotment stay on a reserve list. When banks report more vacancies, IBPS allots from this list. For CSA-XV, a reserve list allotment was issued on 15 June 2026.
  • Allotment is final. IBPS does not entertain requests to change the bank.
  • The allotment is provisional until the bank completes document verification, LLPT where needed, medical test and character verification.

Fill the bank preference order with care. Think about promotion speed, branch network in your home district and transfer policy, not only the bank name.

Common mistakes that cost candidates the job

  • Wrong state: choosing a state for its vacancy count without the language on your Class 10 marksheet.
  • Old OBC certificate: the OBC non-creamy layer certificate must be in the central government format and valid for the period the notice asks for. A state format certificate is often rejected at verification.
  • EWS certificate for the wrong year: it must be for the financial year named in the notice.
  • Bad uploads: blurred photo, signature in capital letters, or a handwritten declaration in a different hand. These lead to cancellation even after you clear Mains.
  • Degree date: the final result must be declared by the closing date. A provisional certificate issued later is not accepted.
  • Careless bank order: filling preferences in the order the list appears.

Keep originals plus two sets of self-attested copies of all these documents ready before the Mains result.

A 10-week plan to the Mains (27 December 2026)

  1. Weeks 1-3: one Reasoning puzzle set and one Quant data interpretation set every day. Read one long English passage daily and note new words.
  2. Weeks 2-8: General and Financial Awareness from July 2026 onward: RBI policy decisions, government schemes, bank mergers, sports and awards. Revise weekly. This section gives 50 marks in only 20 minutes, so it gives the best return on time spent.
  3. Weeks 4-8: two full Mains mocks a week under strict sectional timing. Note which section you leave unfinished.
  4. Weeks 9-10: one mock every alternate day, revise awareness notes, and stop learning new topics.

Frequently asked questions

How many vacancies are there in IBPS Clerk 2026?

The CSA-XVI notification lists 11,403 indicative vacancies across 11 public sector banks. Madhya Pradesh has 570 of these. The final number can change before allotment.

Is there an interview in IBPS Clerk?

No. Selection is through Prelims and Mains only. The final merit list uses Mains marks, followed by document verification and the language test where it applies.

Who has to take the local language test?

Only candidates who cannot show a Class 10 or higher marksheet with the opted state language. The bank conducts it after allotment and before joining, and failing it means no appointment.

Do Prelims marks count in the final result?

No. Prelims is a screening test. You must clear the sectional and overall cut-offs, but the marks are not added to the final merit.

What is the starting salary of an IBPS Clerk?

The starting basic pay is Rs 24,050 under the 12th Bipartite Settlement. With DA, HRA and other allowances, published estimates put take-home pay at roughly Rs 37,000-40,000 a month at the start.

Can I change my state after applying?

No. You apply for one state or UT, and the choice is final. Choose a state whose official language you studied up to Class 10.

When will the IBPS Clerk 2026 Prelims result come?

The Prelims was held on 10 and 11 October 2026. IBPS had not announced a result date as of 16 October 2026. Mains is scheduled for 27 December 2026.
