Latest notice: 2026 cycle

Police Constable posts in Madhya Pradesh are filled by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) for the Police Headquarters, Home (Police) Department. The latest notice, issued on 9 September 2026, is for 7,500 Constable (GD) posts: 700 in the Special Armed Force (SAF), open to men only, and 6,800 in district police (non-SAF), open to men and women.

What the 2025 cycle tells you

The 2025 recruitment was also for 7,500 posts. As per reports quoting MPESB, 9,78,059 people applied and 6,85,504 appeared in the written test. Only 59,438 were called for the physical stage, roughly eight per post. Media reports of the final result in April 2026 showed the unreserved cut-off for the 87% quota near 98, with OBC near 96, EWS near 92, SC near 89 and ST near 80.

Two things stand out. First, the written score decides almost everything, because the physical stage only sees the top few per post. Second, results are split into an 87% quota and a 13% quota. The 13% part is held back for the OBC reservation case pending in court, so a few candidates get a provisional result. Read your scorecard with this in mind.

The cycle also shows how long the process takes. From notice in September 2025 to final result in April 2026 was about seven months, with unit allotment soon after. If the 2026 cycle follows the same pace, expect the final list around mid-2027.

Eligibility and age

  • Education: Class 10 pass (10+2 system) or equivalent for UR, SC and OBC. ST candidates need Class 8 pass.
  • Nationality: Indian citizen. Reservation and age relaxation only for MP domicile holders.
  • Aadhaar: registration and Aadhaar-based biometric checks are mandatory at the centre.

Written exam pattern

The written test is a computer-based objective exam in Hindi and English. It has 100 questions of 1 mark each, a 2-hour slot and no negative marking.

Because the exam runs over many shifts, MPESB normalises scores across shifts. That is why cut-offs come out in decimals like 98.35. A raw 85 in a hard shift may become more than 85 after normalisation, and the reverse in an easy shift. You cannot control this, so aim for accuracy above 90 in mocks.

Topics: MP and India GK, current affairs, history, geography, polity; reasoning, series, analogies, coding; Class 10 level science and arithmetic such as percentage, ratio, time and work. See the syllabus and previous papers.

Physical efficiency test (PET)

The PET carries 100 marks. You need at least 30% to qualify, and reports on the 2026 notice say the final merit uses normalised written marks plus PET marks.

Selection steps

  1. Written exam by MPESB, scores normalised across shifts.
  2. PST and PET by Police Headquarters for candidates in the merit zone.
  3. Document verification of originals. Eligibility is provisional until this step.
  4. Medical examination.
  5. Final merit and unit allotment. In 2025, unit allotment followed the final result within weeks.

After joining, constables go through basic training at a police training school before posting to a district or an SAF battalion.

SAF or district police: which to prefer

Men can be considered for both. District (non-SAF) constables work at police stations, handle beat duty, FIR support, traffic and law and order in a district, and usually stay closer to home. SAF constables serve in armed battalions that move across the state for elections, festivals, VIP duty and Naxal-affected areas, with long periods away from family. SAF has only 700 posts, so its cut-off can behave differently from district police. If you want a stable posting and a later path to Head Constable in a district, give district police the higher preference. If you are fit, single and comfortable with camp life, SAF is a reasonable second choice. Women apply only for district posts.

Common mistakes that cost candidates

  • Women applying for SAF posts. SAF is for men only.
  • ST candidates who are 8th pass claiming the post without a valid ST certificate issued in MP.
  • Name mismatch between Aadhaar and the Class 10 certificate. Biometric checks at entry and DV catch it.
  • Reaching after reporting time. Entry closes at reporting time, not exam time.
  • Training only for running. Long jump and shot put are 60 of the 100 PET marks.
  • Losing the application number, which is needed for every admit card.

Documents to keep ready: Class 8, 10 or 12 marksheets, MP domicile and caste or EWS certificate, Aadhaar, original photo ID, Home Guard or ex-servicemen certificate if claimed, and passport photos.

A 90-day plan for the 2026 exam

Days 1-30: MP GK and current affairs daily, plus arithmetic basics. Start ground work: 2 km easy jog, then 800 m timed runs twice a week.

Days 31-60: reasoning and science chapters, one sectional test a day. Add long jump practice on soft sand and shot put drills three days a week.

Days 61-90: full 100-question mock tests in 2 hours. Time your 800 m weekly against 198 seconds (men) or 262 seconds (women), then push towards full marks.

Keep one rule through all 90 days: never skip both study and ground work on the same day. Candidates who clear the written test often have only a few weeks before the PET, and stamina cannot be built in that gap. Sleep before 11 pm, eat enough protein, and stop running if you feel shin or knee pain, because an injury in the last month costs more than a missed week.

Frequently asked questions

What is the latest MP Police Constable notice?

MPESB issued the Police Constable (GD) 2026 notice on 9 September 2026 for 7,500 posts. The form closed on 6 October 2026 and the written exam starts from 19 November 2026.

What is the minimum education for MP Police Constable?

Class 10 pass for UR, SC and OBC candidates, and Class 8 pass for ST candidates.

Is there negative marking in the written exam?

No. The 100 questions carry one mark each with no deduction for wrong answers.

What is the height requirement?

168 cm for UR, OBC and SC men, 160 cm for ST men and 155 cm for women, as reported for the 2025 and 2026 notices.

Are PET marks added to the merit?

Reports on the 2026 notice say final merit is normalised written marks plus PET marks, with a 30% minimum in PET. Confirm in the official rule book.

Can women apply for SAF constable posts?

No. The 700 SAF posts are for men only. Women can apply for the 6,800 non-SAF posts.
