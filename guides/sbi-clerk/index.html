SBI Clerk 2026 at a glance

SBI recruits its clerical staff directly, outside IBPS. The post is called Junior Associate (Customer Support and Sales). The 2026 cycle runs under advertisement CRPD/CR/2026-27/17, with 7,680 regular vacancies. A separate special drive, CRPD/CR/SPLDRIVE/2026-27/16, fills SC, ST and OBC backlog vacancies, so check which advertisement your application belongs to.

SBI moved its website to sbi.bank.in. All notices, call letters and results for this cycle are on the careers section there.

Selection has three steps: Prelims, Mains and a local language test for those who need it. There is no interview.

Eligibility, age and fee

  • Education: graduation in any discipline. Final-year students can apply if they produce proof of passing by the date SBI sets in the notice.
  • Age: 20-28 years as on 1 April 2026, which means born between 2 April 1998 and 1 April 2006. SC/ST get 5 years, OBC 3 years and PwBD 10 years extra. See age relaxation rules.
  • Fee: Rs 750 for General, OBC and EWS. No fee for SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen.
  • One state only: you can apply for vacancies in one state or UT. You must know its local language.

Note the age cut-off date. SBI uses 1 April, while IBPS Clerk 2026 uses 1 August 2026, so the same person may be eligible for one and over-age for the other. Check with the age calculator.

Exam pattern with sectional timing

Both papers are online and objective, with separate time for each section. One-fourth of the marks for a question is cut for every wrong answer. There is no sectional cut-off in SBI Clerk, which is a real difference from IBPS.

Prelims marks are used only for shortlisting. The final list is made from Mains marks out of 200. Read the information handout with your call letter for any last change.

How the final merit is actually made

  • State-wise, category-wise: SBI shortlists candidates for Mains state by state and category by category. Your competition is only the people who chose your state.
  • No sectional cut-off: you can be weak in one section and still qualify on total. This rewards attempting your strong sections fully.
  • Normalisation: Prelims and Mains run in shifts, so scores are normalised across shifts before the cut-off is applied.
  • Wait list: SBI keeps a wait list and uses it when selected candidates do not join. For the 2025-26 cycle (CRPD/CR/2025-26/06), SBI published a first wait list after the Mains result. Do not give up if your name is not in the first list.

Because there is no sectional cut-off, a good strategy in Mains is to finish General Awareness (50 marks in 35 minutes) with high accuracy and use it to carry the total.

Local language test

If you did not study the local language of your chosen state in Class 10 or Class 12, you must pass a language test after the Mains and before joining. Those who show a Class 10 or 12 marksheet or certificate with that language are exempt.

  • The test, as reported from the notice, has a non-verbal objective part (15 marks), a non-verbal subjective part (15 marks) and a verbal part (20 marks).
  • It is qualifying. Marks are not added to merit.
  • A candidate who fails it is not offered appointment.

For an MP student from a Hindi-medium or CBSE school with Hindi in Class 10, this is a formality. The risk is for candidates who pick a state such as Maharashtra or Gujarat for the larger numbers without the language on their marksheet.

Probation and joining

  • Probation is 6 months. During this time you must complete the e-lessons SBI prescribes. If you do not complete them, probation is extended.
  • Your service is confirmed only after probation, the language test where needed, document checks and medical fitness.
  • You are posted inside the state you applied for. Expect your first branch to be in a semi-urban or rural area.
  • Junior Associates in SBI can later appear in internal promotion exams for officer cadre.

Before joining, keep these ready: graduation marksheets and degree, Class 10 and 12 marksheets (for date of birth and language), caste or EWS certificate in central format, PwBD certificate if any, photo ID, and the printed application form.

Common mistakes in the SBI form

  • Applying to the wrong advertisement: the regular notice and the backlog drive are separate. Reserved candidates should read both.
  • Wrong age date: using the IBPS cut-off date instead of 1 April 2026.
  • Not printing the form: the last date to print was 15 September 2026. You need the print at verification.
  • Category certificate format: OBC must be non-creamy layer in the central format.
  • Uploads: photo, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration must meet the size rules. Rejections happen even after selection.
  • Ignoring email and SMS: call letters, language test details and wait list notices reach the registered email and mobile. Do not apply with a cyber cafe or relative's number.
  • Skipping the pre-exam material: SBI posts an Acquaint Yourself booklet and scribe guidelines with each cycle. Read the booklet once so the screen layout and rules on exam day are not a surprise.

SBI Clerk or IBPS Clerk: which to prefer

Most candidates write both, since the syllabus is the same. If you clear both, compare these points:

  • Pay: SBI starts graduates at Rs 26,730 basic with two increments. IBPS banks start at the scale minimum of Rs 24,050.
  • Selection rule: SBI has no sectional cut-off. IBPS has sectional cut-offs at both stages.
  • Vacancies in MP: this year IBPS has 570 posts for Madhya Pradesh against SBI's 113 regular posts.
  • Brand and network: SBI has the widest branch network, so posting near home is more likely over a career.

Frequently asked questions

How many vacancies are there in SBI Clerk 2026?

The regular notice CRPD/CR/2026-27/17 has 7,680 Junior Associate vacancies. Backlog vacancies for SC, ST and OBC are filled through a separate special drive.

What is the age limit for SBI Clerk 2026?

20-28 years as on 1 April 2026, so you must be born between 2 April 1998 and 1 April 2006. Reserved categories get relaxation as per rules.

Is there a sectional cut-off in SBI Clerk?

No. SBI applies only overall cut-offs, state-wise and category-wise. Only Mains marks count for the final list.

What is the in-hand salary of SBI Clerk?

Starting basic pay is Rs 26,730 and total emoluments are about Rs 46,000 a month in a metro, as reported from the notice. Take-home after NPS and other deductions is lower, and slightly lower again in rural branches because HRA and CCA are lower.

Who has to take the SBI language test?

Candidates who did not study the local language of their chosen state in Class 10 or 12. It is held after Mains and before joining, and failing it means no appointment.

How long is SBI Clerk probation?

Six months. You must complete the prescribed e-lessons in that time or the probation is extended.

When is the SBI Clerk Mains 2026?

SBI has given November 2026 as the tentative month. An exact date had not been announced as of 16 October 2026.
