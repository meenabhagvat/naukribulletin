Latest Government Exam Results 2026

A result is published after each exam stage showing who qualified. This page explains how to check your result the right way, links every major official portal, and updates as results are declared.

How to check your result

  1. Visit the official website of the recruiting body (use the links below).
  2. Open the 'Result' or 'What's New' section on the homepage.
  3. Find the notice for your exam and click the result PDF or login link.
  4. Enter your roll number and date of birth, then download and save your result.
  5. Note your marks and whether you've qualified for the next stage.

What you'll need

  • Registration / Roll number
  • Date of birth or password
  • The exact exam & post name

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my SSC / Railway / Banking result?+

Visit the official website (ssc.gov.in, rrbapply.gov.in, ibps.in), open the Result section, find your exam and download the result PDF or log in with your roll number.

The result PDF won't open — what should I do?+

Results day brings heavy traffic. Try again after some time, or download the PDF and open it offline. Always use the official site, not mirror links.

What is a scorecard vs a result?+

A result shows whether you qualified; a scorecard shows your marks and rank. Scorecards are usually released a few days after the result.

Where can I see the cut-off?+

Cut-offs are published with the result PDF. We also list them on our cut-off page as they are announced.
