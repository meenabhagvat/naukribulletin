How to Check Your Result

Click the View Result button to go directly to the official website. You will need your Registration Number and Date of Birth or Roll Number to check your result. Always check results from official websites only.

Results are declared in stages — Prelims result is declared first, followed by Mains result, and then the Final/Interview result. Shortlisted candidates are called for the next stage. Merit lists and cut-off marks are usually released along with the result.
